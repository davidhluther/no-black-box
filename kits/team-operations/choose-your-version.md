# Choose Your Version

This is the full team version, and it is the only version of this kit. It assumes a team with more than one person, recurring jobs on a shared machine, work that other people depend on and leaders who ask for evidence.

## Why there is no free or low-cost edition

The failures this kit prevents happen in files, folders and schedulers: a job that dies before it writes its status, a conversation that loses a decision when it is summarized, a ledger nobody updated when something shipped. A paste-in prompt in a chat window cannot watch a schedule or read a folder.

## If you are a smaller team or a solo owner

You are not missing the ideas. Two other kits cover the same ground at a smaller scale.

- The [Continuity kit](../continuity/choose-your-version.md) has free and low-cost versions that run on Google Sheets and a chat tool. It asks the question underneath all of this: could the work go on if one person disappeared?
- The [Builder's toolkit](../builders-toolkit/choose-your-version.md) holds the same five operating skills for one person building with Claude Code, without the owner, channel and many-workstream guidance written here.

The sixth skill, `metric-check`, has a free prompt-only form in the [Measure kit](../measure/choose-your-version.md).

## Switching later

Nothing locks you in. The ledger, the briefs and the outcome log are ordinary text files, so they keep their value if you change tools. Adopt one skill first and add the rest later, because each guide stands alone after `TO-FU-01`.
