# Persona Card Template

Each card describes one kind of customer using only evidence you collected. If a field has no evidence, write "not in the evidence" instead of filling it in. Keep a card to 150 words. Date every card and rebuild it when new quotes arrive.

Quote IDs refer to the Quotes tab of your Sheet. This file shows the format with an invented example. The example is made up for illustration and contains no real data.

---

## Card name: [a plain label such as "first-time visitor"]

Date built: [mm/dd/yyyy] | Built from: [number] quotes from [number] sources

**What they are trying to get done.** [One sentence in their words. Cite quote IDs.]

**What worries them.** 
1. [Worry. Quote ID.]
2. [Worry. Quote ID.]
3. [Worry. Quote ID.]

**Words they use for what we offer.** [Short phrases, exactly as quoted.]

**Words that put them off.** [Phrases or tones they react badly to, with quote IDs.]

**What they need to see before acting.** [From the quotes, not from guessing.]

**Not in the evidence.** [List questions the quotes do not answer, so the panel marks guesses instead of inventing.]
