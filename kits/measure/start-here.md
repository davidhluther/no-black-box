# Measure What Matters: Start Here

You will end up with a monthly website report you can trust, built from free Google tools, plus a habit that stops you from believing the wrong number. You will see which visits come from AI tools such as ChatGPT, count the actions that matter on your site (a form sent, a file downloaded, a call started), and know how to check a number before anyone explains it.

## Who it is for

A hair salon with a booking page. A small food bank with a donate button and a volunteer sign-up form. A club, a school or a shop where someone has "the analytics" but nobody is sure what the numbers mean. You do not need a developer, a command line or a budget to start.

## The black box, once

Website numbers tend to arrive in a black box. A dashboard says traffic fell 12 percent, an agency says it was an algorithm change, and nobody shows how they know. Often the tracking was set up by someone who is gone, [event](glossary.md#event) counts are inflated by things that are not visitors, and one tool's number is treated as the truth. Paying an expert to answer "is this number real?" is the common fix. This kit gives you the method so you can answer it yourself.

## What you will have at the end

- A Google Sheet with six tabs that holds your monthly numbers, and a Kit help menu that checks the sheet is set up correctly.
- Tracking for the three to six actions that matter, added through [Google Tag Manager](glossary.md#google-tag-manager) without editing or breaking anything that already works, and tested before anyone sees it.
- A separate line in [GA4](glossary.md#ga4) for visits from AI tools. It is a floor, not a total, and the kit explains why.
- A [Looker Studio](glossary.md#looker-studio) dashboard that redraws itself each month.
- A check you run on every number that moved: define it, confirm it is real, list the possible causes, grade each one holds, weakened or refuted, and confirm the direction on a second independent source.
- A monthly report written from those checks.

## How it works

The kit has six pieces of method, called [skills](glossary.md#skill): `ga4-events-setup`, `metric-check`, `monthly-report`, `looker-export`, `gsc-read` and `clarity-analytics`. In the free version you follow them as guides and paste-in prompts. In the low-cost version a small program in your sheet pulls the numbers for you and one AI plan reads them. In the full version the same skills run in Claude Code and fetch the data themselves.

## What this kit will not do

It will not tell you what to do about your marketing, and it will not promise that AI tools send you visitors. It will tell you what your numbers can and cannot support. When the data cannot answer a question, the report says so.
