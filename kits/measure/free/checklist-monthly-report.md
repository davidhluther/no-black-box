# Checklist: monthly report

Copy into a Google Doc. Date format mm/dd/yyyy.

- [ ] Both months are full calendar months. I wrote the date each source runs through.
- [ ] I exported [Search Console](../glossary.md#search-console) for both months (or ran the Sheets pull).
- [ ] I exported [GA4](../glossary.md#ga4) for both months: organic sessions, engaged sessions, key events, and AI sources.
- [ ] I listed every change in the period with its date: site edits, tracking changes, campaigns, outages, search engine updates.
- [ ] Every number in the table has a source, a filter and a date range.
- [ ] Anything that moved by more than 20% (and at least 100 events) has a flagged-move block with graded causes.
- [ ] Each flagged move was checked on a second independent source, or labeled [single-source](../glossary.md#single-source).
- [ ] No tracking or definition change is shown as a drop or a gain.
- [ ] AI traffic is shown as a floor, with the reason.
- [ ] Things I could not measure are listed as gaps, not estimated.
- [ ] Each finding ends with an action, an owner and a date.
- [ ] The bottom line is three sentences a stranger could follow.
