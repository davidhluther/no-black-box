# CR-LC-01: Make Your Project

You will set up one paid AI plan with a saved [Project](../glossary.md#project) (a saved workspace that keeps your instructions and files so you do not paste them each time). Time: about 30 minutes. Finish the free guides first, so your Sheet already holds quotes.

1. **CR-LC-01-1.** Choose one AI plan. See the [shopping list](../shopping-list.md) for the products and the prices to check. Pick the product you already use. Sign in and subscribe to its paid personal plan from the account or pricing page.
   You'll know it worked when the product shows a Projects option in its left menu. If it does not, you are still on the free plan, so check your account page.

2. **CR-LC-01-2.** Create a new Project and name it "Customer research".
   You'll know it worked when you see an empty Project with a place for instructions and files.

3. **CR-LC-01-3.** Open [instructions.md](../chatgpt/instructions.md) from this kit's chatgpt folder, copy the text between the two lines, and paste it into the Project's instructions box. Then replace the bracketed organization details with the values from your [Config](../glossary.md#config) [tab](../glossary.md#tab).
   You'll know it worked when the box accepts the text. If it refuses because the text is too long, it's E19. Remove the example lines and try again.

4. **CR-LC-01-4.** Upload the [knowledge files](../glossary.md#knowledge-file) listed in [knowledge-files.md](../chatgpt/knowledge-files.md), which are your [prompts](../glossary.md#prompt) file and your [persona](../glossary.md#persona) cards.
   You'll know it worked when the Project lists each uploaded file. Large files may take a minute.

5. **CR-LC-01-5.** Test it. In the Project, type "Remind me how we pull quotes out of reviews" and press Enter.
   You'll know it worked when the answer mentions word-for-word quotes and removing names. If it gives generic advice, instructions did not save, so repeat step 3.

6. **CR-LC-01-6.** Use it each research round. In your Sheet, choose File, then Download, then Comma Separated Values for the Quotes tab, and upload that file to a new chat in the Project. Say "Sort these into [themes](../glossary.md#theme) using the method in your instructions."
   You'll know it worked when you get themes with quote IDs. The free-version [prompts](../free/prompts.md) still work word for word in the Project.

Next, go to [CR-LC-02](CR-LC-02-add-search-suggestions.md).
