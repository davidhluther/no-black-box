# Shopping List

Every account in this list is covered in the shared [keys and accounts guide](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md), which says where to sign up and how to keep your sign-ins private. Nothing here needs an [API key](glossary.md#api-key) (a password a program uses to talk to another program), so you can ignore the parts of that guide about keys.

## Free version

| Item | What it is | Why you need it | Cost | Cheaper or free alternative | Can you skip it? | Room to choose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Google account | The sign-in for Google's free apps | Holds your Sheet and Forms | $0 | A Google Workspace for Nonprofits account if your organization qualifies (the free-for-nonprofits Google package) | No | Use your organization's account so the Sheet outlives any one volunteer |
| Google Sheets | Google's free spreadsheet app | Holds quotes, [themes](glossary.md#theme), and tests | $0 | Excel or Numbers work, but the [check-up](glossary.md#check-up) button needs Google Sheets | No | None |
| A free [AI model](glossary.md#ai-model) plan | A free ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini account | Sorts quotes and plays the simulated panel | $0 | Any one of the three | No | Pick the one you already use; compare answers from two if a result looks odd |
| Google Forms | Google's free survey tool | Asks real customers the follow-up questions in guide 04 | $0 | Paper cards at the counter | Yes, if you ask in person | Use paper if your customers are mostly offline |
| A phone voice-memo app | Records a short chat with a customer | Captures real words from a five-minute conversation | $0, already on your phone | A notebook | Yes | Always ask permission before recording |

## Low-cost version

Everything above, plus the items below.

| Item | What it is | Why you need it | Cost | Cheaper or free alternative | Can you skip it? | Room to choose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| One paid AI plan | Claude Pro, ChatGPT Plus, or the paid Gemini plan | Lets you save a [Project](glossary.md#project) that remembers your method and your words, and handles longer pastes | Claude Pro: $20 a month, or $17 a month billed yearly (checked 11/13/2026). ChatGPT and Gemini: see the current price at [chatgpt.com/pricing](https://chatgpt.com/pricing) and [gemini.google](https://gemini.google/subscriptions/) | The free plan, in the free version | Yes, that is the free version | Pay for one month, run your research round, and cancel if you do not need it again |
| [Apps Script](glossary.md#apps-script) | A tool inside Google Sheets that runs small programs | Pulls search suggestions into your Sheet and records every run | $0, included with your Google account | Typing suggestions in by hand (guide CR-FR-02 shows how) | Yes | None |
| [Reddit](glossary.md#reddit) account | A sign-in for the forum site | Lets you read some forum pages that ask you to sign in | $0 | Reading public pages without signing in | Yes | Skip it unless a page you need requires it |

## What you do not need

You do not need a paid scraping service, a social-listening subscription, or a survey platform. Those are sold to organizations with a research department. A reader of the public web and a spreadsheet covers the same ground at your size.
