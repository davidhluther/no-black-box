# Monthly Board Packet Checklist

Paste this into a Google Doc named "Monthly board packet checklist". Copy the Doc each month, or reset the checkboxes (in Docs, a checklist item is Format > Bullets and numbering > Checklist). The days before the meeting are examples. Set your own.

The agenda is the master list. If the agenda names a report, that report is expected. If a standard report did not arrive, say so in the email. Never leave it out silently.

## Two roles

The secretary runs the checklist. The reviewer (the executive director, a board officer, or any second pair of eyes) reads the email draft before it goes out. If one person does both jobs, wait an hour between writing and reading.

## Before the meeting

About two weeks out

- [ ] Open the Month tab of the tracker sheet (the Google Sheet from guide BP-FR-02). Fill in the meeting date, the month folder name, and the prior meeting's date.
- [ ] Open the Reports tab. Clear last month's "Received this month" column.
- [ ] Ask the chair for the agenda. Add any one-time report the agenda names as a new row on the Reports tab.
- [ ] Remind each report owner of the due date.

About five days out

- [ ] Put each report in the month folder, named so the filename holds its keyword.
- [ ] Mark each one "Yes" under "Received this month". The Month tab then lists what is missing.
- [ ] Contact anyone who has not sent a report.

About three days out

- [ ] Turn last meeting's transcript (the written record of what was said) into draft minutes (prompts 1 through 3 in prompts.md). Put the finished minutes in this month's folder.
- [ ] Write the digest (a short record of the facts the minutes leave out) of last meeting using the fourth prompt (a block of instructions) in prompts.md, pasted into an AI model (a program such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini). Save it in the private transcripts folder.
- [ ] Combine the packet (see the setup guide for your version).
- [ ] Update your contact list if anyone joined or left.

Two days out

- [ ] Write the email draft from email-template.md (prompt 5 helps).
- [ ] Open every link in a private browser window. If you share by name, each link should open after you sign in with a board member's address. If you share with anyone who has the link, no link should ask you to sign in. Fix the sharing first if either is wrong.
- [ ] Ask the reviewer to read the draft. They check recipients, names, motions, attendance, and the missing-reports note.
- [ ] Send the email from Gmail after the reviewer approves it.

## After the meeting

- [ ] Put the recording or transcript in the private transcripts folder, never in a month folder.
- [ ] Make a note of every motion and vote while you remember them.
- [ ] Put a reminder on your calendar to write these minutes during next month's packet run. They go in that month's folder, because that is the packet where the board approves them.
- [ ] Save the sent email as a PDF in the month folder if your board keeps one (Gmail menu > Print > Save as PDF). Leave out the Bcc (hidden recipients) list, because the month folder is shared.

## Distributed separately

Reports you do not want inside the combined packet, such as financials, get their own link in the email. Set "In combined packet" to No on the Reports tab. Every file should be either inside the combined packet or linked by name in the email.

## Standing rules

- The email is always a draft until a person sends it.
- Everything goes as a Drive link, never an attachment. Attachments hit size limits and cannot be updated after sending.
- Full board addresses go in Bcc.
- Minutes go in the following month's folder (the January meeting's minutes ride in the February packet).
