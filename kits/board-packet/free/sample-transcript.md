# Sample Transcript for Practice

This is an invented, short meeting for testing your prompts. Every name, organization, and number is made up. Paste it where a real [transcript](../glossary.md#transcript) would go and check that the minutes you get back match it.

```text
Alex Rivera 00:00:12
Good afternoon, everyone. It's twelve past noon, so let's get started. I see we have a quorum with six of our nine voting members here.

Alex Rivera 00:00:31
First item is the minutes from December. Pat, you had a correction?

Pat Morgan 00:00:38
Yes. Under the grants report, it says the playground grant was for eight thousand dollars. It was eighteen thousand.

Alex Rivera 00:00:49
Thanks, Pat. With that correction, do I have a motion to approve the December minutes?

Robin Shah 00:00:57
I move that we approve the December minutes as corrected.

Pat Morgan 00:01:02
Second.

Alex Rivera 00:01:05
All in favor? ... Any opposed? Then the minutes are approved as corrected.

Sam Okafor 00:01:20
For the director's report. We served about four hundred households in December, up from three hundred sixty in November. The walk-in freezer is failing. The repair quote is twenty-two hundred dollars and I'd like the board's okay to go ahead.

Robin Shah 00:02:10
Is that in the budget?

Sam Okafor 00:02:14
It isn't, but we have a reserve line for equipment.

Robin Shah 00:02:22
Then I move that we approve up to twenty-five hundred dollars from the equipment reserve for the freezer repair.

Pat Morgan 00:02:31
Second.

Alex Rivera 00:02:34
All in favor? Aye. Opposed? One no, from Chris. Motion carries, five to one.

Casey Martin 00:03:05
Quick grants update. We submitted the county community fund application on the fifth. The decision should come in March. I'll send the board a summary by February fifteenth.

Alex Rivera 00:03:40
Great. Any other business? Hearing none, I'll take a motion to adjourn.

Robin Shah 00:03:46
So moved.

Pat Morgan 00:03:48
Second. 

Alex Rivera 00:03:50
We're adjourned at twelve forty-five.
```

Attendance for this practice meeting: Board members present were Alex Rivera (Chair), Pat Morgan, Robin Shah, Chris Lowell, Dana Whitfield, and Lee Park. Staff present were Sam Okafor and Casey Martin.

## What a correct result should show

- The December minutes were approved as corrected. The playground grant is eighteen thousand dollars, not eight thousand.
- The freezer repair vote carried five to one, with Chris voting no, for up to twenty-five hundred dollars from the equipment reserve.
- A grant decision is expected in March, and a summary is due to the board by February 15.
- The meeting ended at 12:45 pm.

A good checking prompt (Prompt 3) will catch it if a draft says the vote was unanimous.
