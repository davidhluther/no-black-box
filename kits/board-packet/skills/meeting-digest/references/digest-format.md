# Meeting Digest Format

A digest (a short, structured record of one meeting) is written from the transcript (the written record of what was said) and compared with the final minutes. It is a finding aid. It is never the source. Where it and the transcript disagree, the transcript wins.

## File header

```
# Meeting digest, [Month D, YYYY] board meeting

> This digest is a derivative index, not a source. It was written by an AI model from the transcript. Check any fact against the transcript before you cite it. Where this digest and the transcript disagree, the transcript wins.

Transcript: [file name or location]
Chat log: [file name, or none kept]
Minutes compared: [file name]
```

## Sections, in this order

1. **Decisions and motions.** Each decision, with who moved, who seconded, and the result. Quote the wording as spoken when it matters. Say if the transcript shows a "no" vote the minutes do not.
2. **Numbers, money, and outside parties.** Every dollar amount, count, percentage, funder, partner, and vendor named. Quote figures as spoken with a few words of context. Note the status of anything pending, such as "applying" or "awaiting a decision". Flag figures that sat in a garbled stretch.
3. **Commitments, deadlines, and follow-ups.** Who said they would do what, and by when.
4. **In the transcript but not in the minutes.** A numbered list of facts from sections 1 to 3 that the final minutes do not carry. This is the section that earns the digest its keep, because minutes are shorter than the meeting by design.
5. **Coverage notes.** How the meeting ran, in a line. Where the transcript quality was poor, with the first few words of the passage so it can be found. Any place the minutes and transcript seem to disagree.

## Rules

- Do not backfill the minutes with facts from the digest. Minutes stay short. The digest carries the rest.
- Quote numbers as heard. Do not round or tidy them.
- Leave out names and details that could identify anyone the organization serves.
- Never invent a fact. If you are not sure, say "unclear" and give the first words of the passage.
- Store the digest beside the transcript, in the private folder, never in a shared month folder.
