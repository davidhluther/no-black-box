# Board Packet Email Template

Copy the text between the lines into a new Gmail message, then replace every [square bracket]. The low-cost version fills most of it in for you.

**Recipients.** To is the chair. Cc is the staff who wrote reports. Bcc (blind carbon copy, which hides addresses from each other) is the full board. Bcc keeps addresses private and stops accidental reply-all.

**Subject.** [Org name] Board of Directors Meeting, [M/D/YY] at [start time]

---

[Org name] Board of Directors and Staff,

Please find the read-ahead materials for the [weekday, month day, year] board meeting at [start to end time]. [We will be in person at the place / We will meet on video.] Video details are at the end of this email.

[One or two sentences about anything special. For example, which item needs a vote, or where an email vote from last month is written up. Keep it warm and short.]

[If any standard report did not arrive: "The [report name] did not arrive in time for this send, so it is not in this month's packet. All other materials appear as normal."]

**For approval or attention this month**
- [Item that needs a vote, with its own link]
- [A one-time item, with its own link]

**Reports and documents**
[One sentence the board hears every month, such as asking everyone to check their contact details.]
- [Combined packet, one file, easy to print]
- [Files folder, every individual document]
- [Each separate report, such as financials, with its own link]
- [Board contact list]
- [Any standing link, such as a volunteer sign-up sheet]

[Optional standing roster, such as who is bringing food or greeting next. Name any open slot as "open" so it gets filled.]

[Video link, meeting ID, and passcode]

[Your name]

---

## Rules that keep the email useful

- Above "Reports and documents" go only items specific to this meeting. A vote, a one-time report.
- Under "Reports and documents" go the standing links.
- Every file is in the combined packet or has its own named link. No file should be reachable only by digging in the folder.
- The video details are standing text. Change them only when the link changes.
- Keep the tone brief and warm.
