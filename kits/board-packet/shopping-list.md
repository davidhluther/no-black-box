# Shopping List

Everything the kit uses, what it costs, and whether you can do without it. Every account or key below is explained step by step in the shared guide [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md). Prices were checked on the vendor's own page on 11/13/2026. They change, so confirm before you buy.

## Free version

For the minutes, typed notes are the dependable free route. Recording transcripts depend on your meeting tool's plan and settings, and the table below shows what each one needs.

| Item | What it is | Why you need it | Cost | Cheaper or free option | Can you skip it? | Room to choose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Google account | A sign-in for Google's online tools. See [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md) | Holds the Drive folders, the Docs, the [tracker sheet](glossary.md#tracker-sheet), and Gmail | [Google Workspace for Nonprofits](glossary.md#google-workspace-for-nonprofits) is $0 per user per month (google.com/nonprofits, checked 11/13/2026) | A free personal Google account works for every step | No | Nonprofit edition if you qualify and want your own email address, otherwise a personal account |
| Free [AI model](glossary.md#ai-model) account | A sign-in for ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. See [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md) | Turns transcripts into draft minutes and digests | Free plans exist for all three, with usage limits | Writing minutes by hand | Yes, if you only want the folders and checklist | Any of the three. The prompts work the same in each |
| Your meeting [transcript](glossary.md#transcript) | A text record of the meeting | The raw material for minutes | Depends on your meeting tool, see below | Your own typed notes, using [Prompt](glossary.md#prompt) 6 in `free/prompts.md` | No, you need either a transcript or notes | See the two rows below |
| PDF merge route | A way to join PDFs into one file | Makes the combined packet | $0. On a Mac, Finder's Quick Actions, Create PDF (documented by Apple). On Windows, PDFsam Basic (pdfsam.org, free and open source) | Skip the combined PDF and email each report as its own link | Yes, but boards that print the packet like having one file | Pick a tool that runs on your own computer, not a website that makes you upload the files |

### Getting a transcript

| Source | What to know | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Google Meet | Google lists transcripts for Business Standard and higher editions of Workspace (support.google.com/meet, checked 11/13/2026). The free nonprofit edition does not list recording | The nonprofit price for Business Standard is $3.50 per user per month on a one-year commitment, or $4.20 billed monthly (google.com/nonprofits, checked 11/13/2026) |
| Zoom | A free account records to your computer. Zoom's automatic transcript comes from a cloud recording, which needs a paid plan, and sources disagree on whether a local recording includes one | See the current prices at zoom.com/pricing |
| Typed notes | The dependable free route. The secretary types notes at the meeting | Free |
| Whisper (full version) | Free software that turns a recording into text on your own computer | Free, plus a computer that can run it |

## Low-cost version

All of the free version, plus the following.

| Item | What it is | Why you need it | Cost | Cheaper or free option | Can you skip it? | Room to choose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paid AI plan | A monthly plan for ChatGPT or Claude. See [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md) | Higher usage limits, so a full transcript fits in one go | Claude Pro $20 a month billed monthly (claude.com/pricing, checked 11/13/2026). ChatGPT Plus $20 a month (OpenAI help center, checked 11/13/2026). For Gemini, see the current price at Google's own pricing page | Both companies list Projects on their free plans (checked 11/13/2026) | Yes. The free plan works with smaller chunks | ChatGPT or Claude, whichever you already use |
| [AI Project](glossary.md#ai-project) | A saved workspace inside the AI tool | Keeps your style guide and rules, so you stop pasting them | Included in the plan | The free plan's Projects | Yes | Claude Project, ChatGPT Project, or a Custom GPT |
| [Apps Script](glossary.md#apps-script) | Google's free tool for small programs inside Sheets and Drive. See [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md) | Adds the menu that makes folders, PDF copies, and the Gmail draft | $0 | Doing those steps by hand | Yes | Use only the menu items you want |

## Full version

All of the free version, plus the following. Low cost is not required first.

| Item | What it is | Why you need it | Cost | Cheaper or free option | Can you skip it? | Room to choose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Claude Code](glossary.md#claude-code) | A program that runs Claude on your own computer and lets it work with files you choose. See [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md) | Runs the five skills | See the current price at claude.com/pricing | The low-cost version | Yes, for most boards | Needs a paid Claude plan or account |
| [Drive for desktop](glossary.md#drive-for-desktop) | A free Google app that shows Drive as a folder on your computer | Lets the tool read and write the month folders | $0 | Google Drive and Gmail connectors in Claude Code | Yes | Folder sync or connectors |
| [LibreOffice](glossary.md#libreoffice) | A free office program | Converts Word and Excel files to PDF | $0 | Exporting each file to PDF yourself | Yes | None needed |
| Whisper and ffmpeg | Free programs that turn a recording into text | Makes a transcript when you only have a recording | $0 | A transcript from your meeting tool | Yes | Model size from small to large, trading speed for accuracy |
| Python add-ons | The packages [pypdf](glossary.md#pypdf) and python-docx | Merge PDFs and write Word files | $0 | None | No, for the full version | None |
| A computer you control | A Mac, Windows, or Linux machine that stays on when you run the tool | Runs the skills | The computer you already own | A low-cost version in the browser | Yes | A laptop is enough |

## What you do not need to buy

A tracker, a scheduler, or a board portal. This kit is the folders, the checklist, the prompts, and a short script, and all of it is yours.
