# Claims Checklist

Run every ad and post through this before you publish. It takes two minutes.

1. Circle every number, date, price, name and comparison in the draft.
2. For each, write where you can show it is true (a receipt, a report, your own records, a public page). If you can't, cut it or turn it into plain opinion.
3. Remove "best," "#1," "guaranteed," "proven," "cure," "free" and "limited time" unless you can back each one today.
4. Check the offer is real, with dates, and matches the [landing page](../glossary.md#landing-page).
5. If you serve a regulated area (health, money and credit, housing, jobs, politics, alcohol, gambling, children), read the platform's ad policy before you write. Start with Google's policy center (https://support.google.com/adspolicy/), Meta's ad standards (https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/) and X's ads policies (https://business.x.com/en/help/ads-policies).
6. For pictures, confirm you own them, have permission, or used a licensed template. Check people shown have given consent.
7. For testimonials, confirm the person agreed in writing and the words are theirs.
8. Read it once aloud. Would a neighbor think you were stretching the truth?
9. Write today's date next to the draft.
