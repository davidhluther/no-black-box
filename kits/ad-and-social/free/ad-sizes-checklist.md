# Ad Sizes and Limits Checklist

Print this. Fill the blank next to each line from the platform's own page on the day you build the ad. Platforms change numbers without notice, so a blank you filled last year is not a fact today.

## Numbers this kit verified on the platform's own page (checked 11/13/2026)

| Platform and field | What the page said | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Google [responsive search ad](../glossary.md#responsive-search-ad) [headline](../glossary.md#headline) | 30 characters each, up to 15 headlines (minimum three when creating an ad) | https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7684791 |
| Google responsive search ad description | 90 characters each, up to four descriptions (minimum two) | same page |
| Google display path | 15 characters each, two path fields | same page |
| Facebook Feed image ad | 4:5 ratio, 1440 x 1800 pixels recommended, JPG or PNG, [primary text](../glossary.md#primary-text) 50 to 150 characters, headline 27 characters (text lengths are recommendations) | https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/image |
| Instagram Feed image ad | 4:5 ratio, 1440 x 1800 pixels recommended, JPG or PNG, primary text 125 characters, headline 40 characters (text lengths are recommendations) | https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/image/instagram-feed |

For Korean, Japanese and Chinese ads, Google counts each character as two.

## How to use it

1. Open the platform's ad screen for the ad type you want.
2. Fill in any blank above that your ad needs.
3. Paste those numbers into the prompt you use.
4. After the draft arrives, count again in the platform's own box. Its counter is the final word.
