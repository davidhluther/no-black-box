# Platform notes for posts

None of these numbers has been verified on a platform's own page, so the skill states none. Open the page, or type a long draft into the platform's composer, and read what it shows today.

| Platform | What changes by platform | Where to look |
|---|---|---|
| X | Post length depends on the account type. The composer shows a counter | https://help.x.com/ and the composer |
| Facebook | Long posts are allowed. Only the beginning shows before "See more" | https://www.facebook.com/help/ |
| Instagram | Caption length, hashtag limits and how much shows before "more" are listed in Help. Links in captions are not clickable | https://help.instagram.com/ |
| LinkedIn | Use the `linkedin-content` skill, or the fallback in `SKILL.md` | https://www.linkedin.com/help/ |

## Free scheduling

Meta Business Suite offers scheduling for Facebook and Instagram posts. Check its help pages for what your account includes and whether anything costs money. X and LinkedIn each have a scheduler in their own composers. This skill never posts for the user.

## One idea, three posts, an invented example

Idea. A neighborhood food pantry needs volunteers to sort donations on Saturday morning.

- X. "Saturday, 9 a.m. We sort donations at Example Pantry and we're short two people. If you can lift a box, come." (Check the length.)
- Facebook. A warmer paragraph naming the street, the door to use, the coffee, and the hour; link to the sign-up page.
- Instagram. First line, "We're two people short on Saturday morning." Then three sentences on what the job is, where to find the sign-up page, and `[hashtags]`.
