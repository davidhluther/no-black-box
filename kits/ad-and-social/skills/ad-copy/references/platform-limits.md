# Platform limits and where to confirm them

Limits change without notice. Use a number below only if it carries a checked date, and confirm it in the ad screen before you rely on it. The ad screen's counter is final.

## Verified on the platform's own page (checked 11/13/2026)

| Platform | Field | What the page said | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Ads | Responsive search ad headline | 30 characters each, up to 15 headlines, at least three needed | https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7684791 |
| Google Ads | Responsive search ad description | 90 characters each, up to four, at least two needed | same |
| Google Ads | Display path | 15 characters each, two fields | same |
| Google Ads | Double-width languages | Korean, Japanese and Chinese characters count as two | same |
| Meta | Facebook Feed image ad text | Primary text 50 to 150 characters, headline 27 characters | https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/image |
| Meta | Instagram Feed image ad text | Primary text 125 characters, headline 40 characters (a "Text Recommendations" section; guidance, not hard limits) | https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/image/instagram-feed |

## Not verified, so look them up

| Platform | What to look up | Where |
|---|---|---|
| X | Post length for the account, ad card text and image shapes | The counter in the X composer; https://business.x.com/en/help/campaign-setup/creative-ad-specifications |
| Instagram | Caption length, hashtag limits, how much of a caption shows before "more" | https://help.instagram.com/ |
| Facebook | Organic post length | The composer shows a warning when you go over |
| Google Ads | Limits for other ad types (call ads, Performance Max, Demand Gen) | https://support.google.com/google-ads/ |
| Meta | Stories, Reels, carousel, video text | https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide |

## Policies

## Re-checking a number

Open the page, find the row, and write today's date beside it. If a page can't be opened or doesn't show the number, say "not verified" and ask the person to read it from the ad screen.
