# Designer Brief Template

Copy this, fill in each line, and send it to a volunteer or designer. Leave nothing blank. If you don't know, write "ask me."

## The job
- **Campaign name.**
- **Where the picture will run** (for example Facebook feed, Instagram story, a search ad's image, an X post).
- **Shape and size.** Use the numbers from the image specs file (`image-specs.md`, in this folder), after you confirm them on the platform's page.
- **File type and size limit.**
- **Deadline.**

## The message
- **One sentence the picture should say.**
- **Who sees it, and where they are in their day.**
- **Words that must appear on the picture, spelled exactly.** If none, write "no text."
- **Call to action, if any.**

## The look
- **Brand colors** (name and hex code, for example #1F6F5C).
- **Fonts.**
- **Logo file and where it goes.**
- **Mood in three words.**
- **Style direction.** Photo, illustration or text on color. Add two links to pictures you like and say what you like about each.

## Keep clear of
- Text covering most of the picture.
- Stock-photo clichés (handshakes, people pointing at laptops).
- Anything that implies a result, a price or an endorsement we can't prove.
- Pictures of identifiable people without written permission.

## Accessibility
- **Alt text** (one plain sentence describing the picture).
- **Contrast.** Text must be easy to read on its background.

## Hand-off
- **What to send back.** For example, two shapes, JPG or PNG, plus the editable file.
- **Who approves it, and by when.**
