# Knowledge Files to Upload

Upload these files from the kit to your ChatGPT [Project](../glossary.md#project) or Custom GPT. ChatGPT allows a limited number of knowledge files, so these are the ones that matter most, in order.

| File | What it gives the [AI model](../glossary.md#ai-model) |
|---|---|
| [free/prompts.md](../free/prompts.md) | The seven prompts and their wording |
| [free/claims-checklist.md](../free/claims-checklist.md) | The honesty check |
| [free/ad-sizes-checklist.md](../free/ad-sizes-checklist.md) | Which numbers were verified, which to look up |
| [overrides/ad-copy/references/platform-limits.md](../skills/ad-copy/references/platform-limits.md) | Limits with checked dates, and policy links |
| [overrides/ad-visuals/references/image-specs.md](../skills/ad-visuals/references/image-specs.md) | Picture shapes with checked dates |
| [free/designer-brief-template.md](../free/designer-brief-template.md) | The brief to fill in |
| [overrides/social-posts/references/platform-notes.md](../skills/social-posts/references/platform-notes.md) | What changes by platform |
| [fixes.md](../fixes.md) | Problem codes, for support |
| [glossary.md](../glossary.md) | Plain definitions |
| Your [voice sheet](../glossary.md#voice-sheet) | Saved from your Voice tab as a text or PDF file. Never upload anything with passwords or keys |
