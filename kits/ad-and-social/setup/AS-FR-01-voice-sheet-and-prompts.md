# AS-FR-01: Build Your Voice Sheet and Try the Prompts

Time about 20 minutes. Cost nothing. You'll finish with a one-page description of how your organization sounds, and your first set of ad text.

1. **AS-FR-01-1.** Open a free chat AI model (ChatGPT at chatgpt.com, Claude at claude.ai or Gemini at gemini.google.com) and sign in. Pick one and stay with it for this guide.
   You'll know it worked when you see a box where you can type a message. If you loop back to the sign-in page, it's E01.
2. **AS-FR-01-2.** Open [prompts.md](../free/prompts.md) and copy Prompt 1. Replace the words in square brackets with your own.
   You'll know it worked when the model replies with a first question. If it writes a [voice sheet](../glossary.md#voice-sheet) without asking anything, add "Ask me one question at a time" and send again (E02).
3. **AS-FR-01-3.** Answer the questions, up to ten. Answer in your own words, short is fine. Say "I don't know" when you don't.
   You'll know it worked when the model says it's ready to write the sheet, or writes it.
4. **AS-FR-01-4.** Read the finished voice sheet. Fix anything that is wrong. Look hard at the lists "Claims we can prove" and "Claims we must never make."
   You'll know it worked when every claim has proof written beside it. If a claim has none, move it to "never make" until you find proof (E14).
5. **AS-FR-01-5.** Copy the voice sheet into a plain document you can find again, such as a Google Doc named "Voice sheet." You'll move it into a Google Sheet in the next guide.
   You'll know it worked when you can open it tomorrow.
6. **AS-FR-01-6.** Start a new chat. Paste the voice sheet, then Prompt 2 from the prompts file with your own offer. Open the platform's ad screen, read the limits it shows today, and fill them in. The [sizes checklist](../free/ad-sizes-checklist.md) says where to look.
   You'll know it worked when you get [headlines](../glossary.md#headline) with character counts after each one and a claims list. If the model goes past your limits, tell it "Line three is over the limit, shorten it" (E03). If your numbers don't match the checklist, trust the ad screen (E04).
7. **AS-FR-01-7.** Count three of the headlines yourself by pasting them into the ad screen. Delete any claim that is marked "not from my voice sheet" or add its proof to the sheet. Run the [claims checklist](../free/claims-checklist.md).
   You'll know it worked when the ad screen accepts the text and the checklist is complete.

> **How to get help.** Write down the step ID where you got stuck (it looks like AS-FR-01-3). If your kit has a check-up button or command, run it and copy the support code it prints. Then send both to Pigasus Group at https://pigasus.group/contact. Setup questions are free.
