# AS-LC-02: Use Canva With Your AI Plan

Time about 20 minutes. [Canva](../glossary.md#canva) remains the place pictures get made. The AI plan writes the instructions and the [alt text](../glossary.md#alt-text).

1. **AS-LC-02-1.** Sign in to Canva. If your AI vendor offers a Canva connection (a link that lets the [AI model](../glossary.md#ai-model) open Canva for you), look for it in the vendor's connections or apps settings. This is optional, and availability changes, so read the vendor's page first.
   You'll know it worked when Canva shows as connected. If you don't see it, skip it and work by hand, which is the same as the free version (E05).
2. **AS-LC-02-2.** In your [Project](../glossary.md#project), ask "Give me three picture ideas for [ad or post] in [shape]." Use the shape from the [sizes checklist](../free/ad-sizes-checklist.md), after you've checked the platform page.
   You'll know it worked when each idea has Canva search words, a layout, brand colors and alt text.
3. **AS-LC-02-3.** Build the picture in Canva as in guide AS-FR-03, steps 3 to 6.
   You'll know it worked when the file opens on your phone.
4. **AS-LC-02-4.** Ask the Project "Check this ad and picture description against my claims list" and paste the draft.
   You'll know it worked when each claim is labeled and unsupported ones come with a rewrite (E14).

> **How to get help.** Write down the step ID where you got stuck (it looks like AS-LC-02-2). If your kit has a check-up button or command, run it and copy the support code it prints. Then send both to Pigasus Group at https://pigasus.group/contact. Setup questions are free.
