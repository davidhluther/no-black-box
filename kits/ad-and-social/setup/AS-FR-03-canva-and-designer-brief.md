# AS-FR-03: Make Pictures With Free Canva or a Designer Brief

Time about 30 minutes. You'll finish with one finished picture at the right shape, or a brief a volunteer can act on.

1. **AS-FR-03-1.** Open [canva](../glossary.md#canva).com and sign up or sign in with the free plan. Canva is a design tool in your web browser with ready-made layouts.
   You'll know it worked when you see Canva's home screen with a "Create a design" button. A sign-in loop is E01.
2. **AS-FR-03-2.** In a new chat, paste your [voice sheet](../glossary.md#voice-sheet) and Prompt 6 from [prompts.md](../free/prompts.md), with the shape from the [sizes checklist](../free/ad-sizes-checklist.md).
   You'll know it worked when you get three picture ideas with Canva search words and [alt text](../glossary.md#alt-text).
3. **AS-FR-03-3.** In Canva choose "Create a design," search the shape you need (for example "Instagram post" or "Facebook ad"), and pick a template matching the first idea. Swap the words and colors for yours. Confirm the picture's pixel size on the platform page. Canva's size panel shows the current size.
   You'll know it worked when the page size in Canva matches the platform's number. If it doesn't, it's E05.
4. **AS-FR-03-4.** Keep the picture simple. Use short text, large type and one idea. Don't use text you can't read on a phone.
   You'll know it worked when you can read it at arm's length on your phone.
5. **AS-FR-03-5.** Add alt text. In Canva, open the picture's options and write the alt text from step 2. If the platform asks for alt text when you post, paste it there instead.
   You'll know it worked when the alt text is one plain sentence saying what the picture shows.
6. **AS-FR-03-6.** Download as JPG or PNG. If the file is larger than the platform allows, choose a smaller quality setting.
   You'll know it worked when the file opens on your phone.
7. **AS-FR-03-7.** If you'd rather hand the picture to someone else, copy [designer-brief-template.md](../free/designer-brief-template.md), fill every line and send it.
   You'll know it worked when the person can start without asking you a question.
8. **AS-FR-03-8.** Skim the picture for garbled words, unlicensed logos, or people who didn't agree to be shown (E08), then run the [claims checklist](../glossary.md#claims-checklist) one last time.
   You'll know it worked when you'd be comfortable showing it to a neighbor.

> **How to get help.** Write down the step ID where you got stuck (it looks like AS-FR-03-3). If your kit has a check-up button or command, run it and copy the support code it prints. Then send both to Pigasus Group at https://pigasus.group/contact. Setup questions are free.
