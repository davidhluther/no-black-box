# Glossary

Every technical word used in this kit, in plain terms.

**A/B test.** Running two versions of an ad at the same time to see which gets more of what you want, like selling lemonade at two prices on the same street on the same day.

**AI model.** The program behind ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini that writes text from your instructions.

**Alt text.** A short written description of a picture, read aloud to people who can't see it and shown when a picture won't load.

**API key.** A long secret code that proves a request comes from your account, like a hotel key card. Whoever holds it can spend your money.

**Apps Script.** Google's built-in way to add small helper programs to a Google Sheet, such as the Help menu in this kit.

**Aspect ratio.** The shape of a picture as width to height. 4:5 is a tall rectangle, 1:1 is a square.

**Call to action.** The one thing you ask the reader to do next, such as "Sign up" or "Call us."

**Canva.** A free online design tool with ready-made layouts for pictures.

**Character limit.** The largest number of letters, spaces and punctuation marks a field accepts.

**Claims checklist.** The two-minute list in this kit for confirming each statement in an ad is true and provable.

**Claude Code.** A program you control by typing, which can read and write files on your computer and follow skills.

**Config file.** A small settings file (here `config.json`) that holds your organization's details in one place.

**Control.** The plain version of an ad that other versions are compared against.

**CSV.** A plain text table where commas separate the columns. Google Sheets can open and paste it.

**Designer brief.** A one-page written request that tells a designer what the picture needs to do, how it should look and what to avoid.

**Environment variable.** A named setting your computer remembers outside your files, used to keep secret keys out of documents.

**Google Sheets.** Google's free spreadsheet app, which runs in your web browser.

**Hashtag.** A word with a # in front that groups posts on one topic.

**Headline.** The short line at the top of an ad.

**Image service.** A company's computers that draw a picture from your written description, charging per picture.

**JSON.** A way to write settings as plain text, with names and values in braces. This kit uses it only for `config.json`.

**Landing page.** The web page a person arrives at after clicking your ad or post.

**Marketplace.** A catalog of add-ons that Claude Code can install, like an app store with one shelf.

**Placement.** The exact spot an ad appears, such as a Facebook feed or an Instagram story.

**Primary text.** The main message above a Facebook or Instagram ad's picture.

**Project.** A saved workspace in an AI plan where the model keeps your files and instructions between chats.

**Responsive search ad.** A Google search ad where you supply several headlines and descriptions and Google mixes them.

**Skill.** A named set of instructions the AI model follows for one job, such as writing ad copy.

**Terminal.** A window where you type commands instead of clicking, the program on your computer that runs them.

**Variant.** One version of an ad that differs from the control by a single change, so you can see what that change does.

**Voice sheet.** The one-page description of how your organization sounds, what it offers and what it must never claim.
