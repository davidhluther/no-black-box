Prices are not printed here because they change. Where a row says "see the vendor's page," that page is the only source we trust.

## About the keys

A key is like a hotel room key card. Whoever holds it can use your account and spend your money. The keys and accounts guide shows where to store it (in an [environment variable](glossary.md#environment-variable), which is a setting your computer remembers outside the files you share) and how to keep it out of screenshots. Never paste a key into a chat, a sheet, or this kit's files.

## What the kit never buys

It never signs you up for anything, posts for you, or spends ad money. You do those steps yourself in each platform's own screens.
