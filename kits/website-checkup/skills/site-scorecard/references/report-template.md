# Report template

Use this shape. Keep it to two pages of reading. Details go in the sheet or appendix.

```
# Website check-up: [business name]
Checked [mm/dd/yyyy]. Business type: [type]. Area: [city or region].
Competitors compared: [names].

## Five things to know
1. [Headline, one sentence, with its evidence.] Confidence: [Confirmed/Likely/Unverified]
2. ...

## Scores by section
| Section | Points | Possible | Percent | Checked of total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
(Google listing, reviews, speed, structured data, local search, positioning, search visibility)

## Marketing baseline
[Done count] done, [Needs done count] needs done, [N/A count] not applicable. List the items that need work, in phase order.

## Business-type extras
[Short list.]

## Fixes, in order
[Hand off to the action-plan skill, or list the top five with effort (small, medium, large).]

## Investigate
[Hypotheses worth testing, one line each.]

## What this check could not see
[Plain list: dashboard-only data, analytics, private reviews, location-specific rankings, anything marked unknown.]

## Sources
[Each page opened or tool used, with date.]
```

Style rules for the report. Spell out zero to ten, use digits from 11 up. Write dates as mm/dd/yyyy. No scores without a source. No promises about rankings.
