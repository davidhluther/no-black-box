# Business types and their extra checks

Pick the closest type. The competitor radius is a starting point and should be widened for destination businesses and narrowed in dense cities. The extra checks are added to the report after the main sections.

| Type | Radius (miles) | Extra checks |
|---|---|---|
| Restaurant or food and drink | 5 | Menu visible as text (not just a picture or PDF). Reservation or ordering link works. Yelp listing complete. |
| Retail (shop or online) | 10 | Product pages have photos, price, and availability. Inventory or "in stock" visible. Shop-local signals (hours, parking, events). Reviews gathered on more than one platform. |
| Home services (contractors, trades) | 20 | A page per service area. Emergency availability stated. License and insurance displayed. Financing offered and explained. Presence on trade directories. Before-and-after photos. |
| Professional services (law, accounting, advice) | 15 | Named people with bios and credentials. Case studies or client stories (with permission). Practice areas have their own pages. |
| Dental | 10 | Insurance accepted, stated plainly. Online scheduling. |
| Clinical health (medical, therapy, mental health) | 15 | Insurance accepted. Provider bios and credentials. New-patient steps. Online scheduling. Presence on health directories. Accessibility. Flag privacy rules for review by someone qualified, and do not give legal advice. |
| Medical spa or aesthetics | 20 | Before-and-after gallery with consent language. A gallery page per treatment. Alt text on gallery images. Flag claims and licensing rules for review by someone qualified. |
| Real estate | 10 | Listing feed integration works. Neighborhood guides. Agent bios. Video tours. Testimonials on the site. Presence on the big listing portals. |
| Hospitality (inns, rentals, venues) | 15 | Direct booking path. Presence on booking sites and whether rates match across them. Amenity clarity. Photo quality. Seasonal positioning. |
| B2B service (field, technical, compliance) | none | LinkedIn presence. Certifications displayed. Technical content depth. Case studies. Contact page clarity. |
| B2B software | none | Pricing visible. Free trial or demo flow. Integrations page. Docs and changelog. Status page. Customer logos. Review-site presence. |
| Specialty food producer (online and wholesale) | none | Wholesale inquiry path. Shipping and ordering clarity. Local listings for the production site. |
| Other | 10 | Ask the owner what a customer does before choosing. Add two checks that match. |

For businesses with no radius (online, B2B), pick competitors by who appears when the customer's question is searched, not by distance.
