# Civic Voice: Start Here

By the end of this kit you will have a tracker (a free online spreadsheet in Google Sheets) and a folder where every public meeting you follow gets a dated prep memo, a comment that fits your time limit with each fact traced to a page, and a record of what happened. You will also have a records request ready to send under your own state's law, a table of county numbers that shows how uncertain each number is, and a searchable [transcript](glossary.md#transcript) of a meeting video. Nothing here costs money in the free version, and nothing in it takes a side.

## The black box

Public meetings can feel like they run on insider knowledge. Which of the hundreds of pages in the [agenda packet](glossary.md#agenda-packet) matter? What can you say in three minutes? How do you ask for the contract everyone keeps mentioning? Which of the county's numbers can you trust?

Too often the answers go to people who pay a consultant, and everyone else stays home. This kit writes the method down in plain words so a small food bank, a hair salon on a street that is about to change, or a neighbors' association can do it themselves.

## Who it's for

Residents, civic groups, and small nonprofits who want to show up prepared. You do not need any technical background. You need a Google account, about an hour for the free setup, and a meeting you care about.

## What this kit will not do

- It will not pick a side. It helps you say your own view clearly and truthfully, and it works the same whatever your view is.
- It will not send, file, sign up, or speak for you. Everything it writes is a draft you read and send.
- It will not give legal advice. For a denied records request, a conflict-of-interest question, or an appeal, it points you to a lawyer, a legal aid group, or your state's open-government help line.
- It will not make up a law. For anything legal, you look up the official page yourself, and the kit tells you how.

## A note on honesty

AI models can sound sure while being wrong. Every skill here makes the [AI model](glossary.md#ai-model) show the page, [timestamp](glossary.md#timestamp), or source for each fact, and mark what it could not check. The method asks you to check the few facts you will say out loud. That check is the part no tool should do for you.
