# Choose Your Version

All three versions do the same four jobs. They differ in how much a program does for you and how much you do by hand. You can switch at any time. Your tracker, folders, and notes carry over unchanged.

| | Free (FR) | Low cost (LC) | Full (FU) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best for | Following a few meetings a year | Following meetings every month | A group that pulls numbers or transcripts often |
| What you need | A Google account and a web browser | Everything in free, plus one [AI model](glossary.md#ai-model) plan | Everything in low cost, plus a computer where you can use the [terminal](glossary.md#terminal) |
| Cost | Nothing | One AI plan. See the current price at [chatgpt.com/pricing](https://chatgpt.com/pricing), [claude.com/pricing](https://claude.com/pricing), or [gemini.google/subscriptions](https://gemini.google/subscriptions) (checked 11/13/2026) | The low-cost plan or a Claude plan that includes [Claude Code](glossary.md#claude-code) (see the shopping list), plus free tools |
| Setup time | About an hour | About an hour more | An afternoon, once |
| Prep memo and comment | Paste the [packet](glossary.md#agenda-packet) text and a ready-made [prompt](glossary.md#prompt) into a free AI model | The same, saved in a ChatGPT Project or a Claude Project, so you paste less | The [skill](glossary.md#skill) reads your saved packet folder directly |
| Records request | Prompt plus a lookup checklist | The same in a saved Project, plus an [Apps Script](glossary.md#apps-script) date highlighter on your tracker | Same as low cost |
| County numbers | Copy from Census Reporter or data.census.gov into your sheet | The same, with the AI model writing the plain-language sentences | A [script](glossary.md#script) pulls the table, adds the [margin of error](glossary.md#margin-of-error) flags, and checks comparisons |
| Meeting transcripts | Copy the video site's own [transcript](glossary.md#transcript), clean it with a prompt | The same in a saved Project | [yt-dlp](glossary.md#yt-dlp) fetches the [captions](glossary.md#captions) and a script builds the transcript with links to the video |
| Limits | More copying and pasting. Long packets may need splitting. Free AI models limit how much you can paste | Same limits on what the AI model can do, fewer repeated steps | Needs a computer you can install programs on, and a few typed commands |

## Which should I pick?

Start with free if you are not sure. It does the whole job and nothing is locked behind a payment. Move to low cost when the pasting gets tiring. Move to full when you pull county numbers or transcripts often, or when you want a table of ten counties in a minute instead of an afternoon.

## Room to choose

- You can mix. Many groups run free for meetings and records, and add the full version only for the Census table.
- A different free AI model works as well as the one in the guides. The prompts are plain text.
- If you share the work across a group, use a shared Google Drive folder and one shared tracker.

## Switching later

- Free to low cost. Add the saved Project (guide [CV-LC-01](setup/CV-LC-01-save-the-kit-in-a-project.md)) and, if you like, the date highlighter ([CV-LC-02](setup/CV-LC-02-add-the-date-highlighter.md)). Keep your tracker as it is.
- Low cost to full. Add the tools in [CV-FU-01](setup/CV-FU-01-set-up-claude-code-and-the-skills.md). Your folders and tracker stay the same, and the skills read the same `config.json`.
- Full back to free. Nothing breaks. The prompts and checklists in the `free/` folder still work.
