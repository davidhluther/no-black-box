# Shopping List

Every item the kit uses, with what it is, why it is needed, what it costs, and a cheaper or free way. Accounts and keys are explained further in the shared [keys and accounts guide](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md). Costs change, so each price is a link to the vendor's page, not a number copied here.

## Used by every version

**A Google account.** A free login for Google Sheets (a spreadsheet), Docs (a word processor), and Drive (online folders). It holds your tracker and your meeting folders. Cost, free for a personal account. You can use Microsoft Excel and OneDrive instead, but the [check-up](glossary.md#check-up) button and date highlighter are for Google Sheets. You can skip it only if you keep everything on paper. More on accounts in the [keys and accounts guide](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md).

**A web browser.** Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox. It opens the agenda pages, Census Reporter, and video sites. Free. Any current browser works.

**A free [AI model](glossary.md#ai-model).** A chat tool such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. It reads your pasted packet and drafts memos and comments. Cost, free tiers exist, but their limits change, so see the current limits on each vendor's page ([ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/pricing), [Claude](https://claude.com/pricing), [Gemini](https://gemini.google/subscriptions)). You can choose any of them. You can skip it if you are willing to write the memo and comment yourself with the checklists. Account setup is in the [keys and accounts guide](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md).

**The meeting body's own web pages.** The agenda page, the comment rules page, and the video page. They are where the official documents live. Free. No substitute, since a news story is not the packet.

**A printer, or a library or print shop.** For printed copies of your comment to hand to the [clerk](glossary.md#clerk). Cost depends on where you print. You can skip it if the body accepts written comments by email, or if you read from a screen.

## Records requests

**A way to send that leaves a dated record.** An online form, an email, or certified mail. The record proves when you asked and starts the clock on the office's response. Email and online forms are free. For certified mail, see the current price on the [U.S. Postal Service site](https://www.usps.com). You may choose any method the agency accepts. You can skip certified mail if the agency's form gives you a dated confirmation.

**Your state's public-records law, from an official page.** The kit has you look it up. It is free and has no substitute, since the AI model must not supply it from memory.

## County numbers

**Census Reporter and data.census.gov.** Free websites that show [American Community Survey](glossary.md#american-community-survey) numbers. Census Reporter at [censusreporter.org](https://censusreporter.org) is the easier to read, and data.census.gov at [data.census.gov](https://data.census.gov) is the Census Bureau's own and has every table. Neither needs an account to browse (checked 11/13/2026). Use either one or both. This is the free route, so nothing here is optional unless you use the full version.

**A Census [API](glossary.md#api) key (full version only).** A code that lets the [script](glossary.md#script) ask the [Census Bureau's API](glossary.md#api) for numbers. The Bureau asks for it because its data service sends requests without one to a "missing key" page (tested 11/13/2026). You request it at [api.census.gov/data/key_signup.html](https://api.census.gov/data/key_signup.html). The signup form asks for an organization name and an email address, asks you to agree to the terms, and shows no payment step (checked 11/13/2026). You can skip it if you use the free route. Keep it out of shared files and screenshots; see the [keys and accounts guide](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md).

## Meeting transcripts

**The video site's own [transcript](glossary.md#transcript).** On YouTube and some other sites, a "Show transcript" button sits under the video description. Free. Not every video has one. Skip the full route if it exists.

**[yt-dlp](glossary.md#yt-dlp) (full version only).** A free, open-source program that fetches caption files. Free to download from [github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp). The free route replaces it.

## Low cost version

**One AI plan.** A paid plan from ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. It gives you saved Projects (or a [Custom GPT](glossary.md#custom-gpt)) so the kit's instructions are loaded once. See the current price on each vendor's page: [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/pricing), [Claude](https://claude.com/pricing), [Gemini](https://gemini.google/subscriptions) (checked 11/13/2026). You can choose any one. You can skip it by staying on free. Account setup is in the [keys and accounts guide](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md).

**Google [Apps Script](glossary.md#apps-script).** Built into Google Sheets. Free. It adds the date highlighter. You can skip it.

## Full version

**A computer you can install programs on.** A Mac, Windows, or Linux computer. You already own one or you borrow one. Cost, none new. You can skip the full version if the free route is enough.

**[Python](glossary.md#python) 3.** A free programming language the scripts need. Get it from [python.org/downloads](https://www.python.org/downloads/). Many Macs already have it. See guide [CV-FU-01](setup/CV-FU-01-set-up-claude-code-and-the-skills.md).

**[Claude Code](glossary.md#claude-code).** Anthropic's program that runs on your computer and uses the kit's skills. Most ways of using it need a Claude subscription or an Anthropic Console account (checked 11/13/2026 on the [Claude Code overview page](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/overview)). See the current prices at [claude.com/pricing](https://claude.com/pricing). You can skip it and still run the two scripts by hand, since they need only Python. Account setup is in the [keys and accounts guide](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md).
