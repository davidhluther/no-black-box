# Comment templates

All text below is invented example material. Replace the brackets with the speaker's own words and facts.

## Spoken comment, about three minutes

"Good evening. My name is [name], and I live at [address if required]. I am speaking [for / as a member of] [group] on item [number], [title].

I am asking the [body] to [one specific request, in a sentence].

Here is what I found in the packet. On page [page], [fact]. On page [page], [fact]. [Optional third fact.]

[One sentence from the speaker's own experience, in the speaker's own words. For example: 'I walk this block every morning, and the crossing at [street] has no signal.']

So my request is [restate the ask in the same words]. If it would help, I can share [handout or source] with the clerk (the staff member who keeps a body's official records). Thank you for your time."

## Thirty-second version

"My name is [name]. On item [number], I am asking the board to [ask]. The packet shows [fact] on page [page]. Thank you."

## Written version for the clerk

Subject: Written comment for [mm/dd/yyyy] meeting, item [number]

[Name, group, and how to reach you.]

To the members of [body],

I am writing about item [number], [title]. I ask the [body] to [ask].

[The two or three facts with packet pages, as in the spoken comment, with a little more detail.]

Please include this comment in the record for the meeting, and let me know if another step is needed. Thank you.

[Name]

## Things to leave out

- Anything about why a named person acted as they did.
- Numbers you did not check against the page.
- Private details about neighbors.
- Predictions stated as facts. Say "I am concerned that" when you mean a worry.
