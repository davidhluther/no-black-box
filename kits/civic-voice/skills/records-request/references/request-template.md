# Request and follow-up templates

Everything below is invented example text. Replace the brackets with your own details and the answers from your state-law lookup.

## Cover note for the user (not part of the letter)

- Who is asking: [person or group].
- Law cited: [name and section], from [link], checked [mm/dd/yyyy].
- Response deadline: [rule], so about [mm/dd/yyyy] if sent on [mm/dd/yyyy]. Counted as [business or calendar] days.
- Open decisions: [list].

## Request letter

To: [Records officer or Clerk (the staff member who keeps a body's official records)], [Agency]
From: [Name, group if any], [mailing address if required], [phone], [email]
Date: [mm/dd/yyyy]
Subject: Public records request, [short subject]

Hello,

I am writing [on behalf of group / as a resident of place] to request copies of public records under [name and section of the state law].

I am looking for these records, for the period [start date] through [end date]:

1. [Record type, subject, office or role.] Example: Final or draft reports, memos, or emails by [office] about [project].
2. [Record type.] Example: The contract, change orders, and invoices for [project name].
3. [Record type.] Example: Maps or data files showing [feature] in [area].

Electronic copies are preferred and are fine in the formats you already have. If any part of this request would cost more than $[amount], please send an estimate first and I will confirm or narrow it. [If the law allows: Please tell me whether a fee waiver is available for requests in the public interest.]

If any record is withheld, please tell me which part and cite the exemption (a reason in the law that lets an office keep a record back) you rely on, and release the rest. I am glad to narrow the request if that makes it easier. You can reach me at [contact].

Thank you for your help.

[Name]

## Review checklist

- [ ] Every item is a record, not a question.
- [ ] Each item has a record type, a subject, and a date range.
- [ ] No request for private people's personal details.
- [ ] The citation matches the lookup table.
- [ ] The tone has no accusation or argument.
- [ ] The contact details are right and the sending method leaves a dated record.

## Follow-up: no response by the deadline

Subject: Follow-up on public records request sent [mm/dd/yyyy]

Hello,

I sent the request below on [mm/dd/yyyy] and have not yet received a response. Under [law and section], I understand a response is due by [mm/dd/yyyy]. Could you tell me where the request stands and when I can expect the records?

Thank you.
[Name]

## Reply to a fee estimate

Hello,

Thank you for the estimate of $[amount]. [I would like to proceed. / Please send items 1 and 2 first, and I will narrow item 3 to [new scope]. / Could you tell me what part of the cost is staff time, and whether a narrower date range would lower it?]

Thank you.
[Name]

## Reply to partial release

Hello,

Thank you for sending [items]. I did not receive [items]. Could you tell me whether those records are being prepared, withheld, or do not exist? If withheld, please cite the exemption for each.

Thank you.
[Name]

## Reply to a denial

Hello,

Thank you for your response of [mm/dd/yyyy]. I would like to understand the basis for withholding [item]. Could you give me the specific exemption you relied on, whether any part of the record can be released with redactions, and the steps for requesting a review?

Thank you.
[Name]

(Before an appeal, talk to your state's open-government help line, a legal aid group, or a lawyer. This template is not legal advice.)
