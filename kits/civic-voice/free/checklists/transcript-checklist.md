# Meeting transcript checklist

Copy into a Google Doc.

- [ ] Find the video on the body's own page or channel. Save the link, meeting date, and body.
- [ ] Save the agenda too (it has the correct name spellings).
- [ ] Open the platform's [transcript](../../glossary.md#transcript) (setup guide CV-FR-05). Copy it with timestamps.
- [ ] Run [prompt](../../glossary.md#prompt) 7 in `prompts.md`. Save the result with the notice at the top.
- [ ] Spot-check three timestamps against the video, including one near the end.
- [ ] Before quoting anyone, play the video at the [timestamp](../../glossary.md#timestamp) and confirm the words.
- [ ] When the [adopted minutes](../../glossary.md#adopted-minutes) are posted, compare any decisions in the transcript with them.
- [ ] Add a row to the Transcripts tab.
