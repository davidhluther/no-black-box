# Meeting prep checklist

Copy into a Google Doc. Check boxes as you go. Dates are mm/dd/yyyy.

## A week before

- [ ] Find the official agenda page and save the agenda and packet to a dated folder: `yyyy-mm-dd-body/source/`.
- [ ] Write down where you got them and the date and time you retrieved them.
- [ ] Find the comment rules: sign-up deadline, time limit, which items you may speak on, and whether written comments are accepted. Write the source link next to each rule. If you cannot find one, ask the [clerk](../../glossary.md#clerk).
- [ ] Add a row to the Meetings tab.

## Four days before

- [ ] Paste the packet into an [AI model](../../glossary.md#ai-model) with [prompt](../../glossary.md#prompt) 1 in `prompts.md`. Save the memo as `prep-memo-yyyy-mm-dd`.
- [ ] Open the governing document (zoning code, policy, or law) and run prompt 2 on your planned asks.
- [ ] Mark each fact in your memo as "verified in packet page ___" or "unverified."

## Two days before

- [ ] Check for an updated packet or agenda change.
- [ ] Write the comment (prompt 3). Read it aloud with a timer. Cut to the time limit minus a margin.
- [ ] Confirm the sign-up method and deadline.

## Day of

- [ ] Sign up by the deadline.
- [ ] Bring printed copies of your comment and any one-page handout.
- [ ] Bring your memo, with page numbers marked.
- [ ] Plan for a long meeting: if the item moves or time runs out, send the written version to the clerk the same day and ask how to have it added to the record.

## Within one day after

- [ ] Write the outcome record (prompt 4). Label it "my notes."
- [ ] Update the Meetings and Comments tabs.
- [ ] Put a reminder to compare with the [adopted minutes](../../glossary.md#adopted-minutes) when posted.
