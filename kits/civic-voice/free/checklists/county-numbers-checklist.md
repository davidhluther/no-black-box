# County numbers checklist

Copy into a Google Doc.

- [ ] Choose the counties, and add your state as a yardstick.
- [ ] Choose at most a dozen measures. (Suggestions in `skills/census-county-table/references/measures.md`.)
- [ ] Choose the five-year estimates. Write down the period (for example 2020 to 2024).
- [ ] Get the numbers from Census Reporter or data.census.gov (setup guide CV-FR-04).
- [ ] Copy the estimate and its [margin of error](../../glossary.md#margin-of-error) into the County numbers tab.
- [ ] Flag any number whose margin of error is more than 30 percent of the estimate.
- [ ] For each comparison, check whether the gap is larger than the square root of the sum of the two squared margins. If not, write "not clearly different."
- [ ] Check two numbers by hand against the source.
- [ ] Write the source line: U.S. Census Bureau, [American Community Survey](../../glossary.md#american-community-survey), [period] 5-year estimates, retrieved [mm/dd/yyyy].
