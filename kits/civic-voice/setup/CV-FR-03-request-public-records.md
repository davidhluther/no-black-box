# CV-FR-03: Request Public Records

Free version, guide 3. You will draft a [public-records request](../glossary.md#public-records-request) (a written ask for copies of records an office already holds) under your own state's law, send it, and track the answer. Allow an hour for the first one. The most important step is looking up the law yourself. An [AI model](../glossary.md#ai-model) can draft the letter, but it must not supply the legal details from memory.

1. **CV-FR-03-1.** Decide who is asking (you or your group) and which agency holds the records. If you are not sure which office, start with the agency's [clerk](../glossary.md#clerk) or records officer. Write one sentence on what you want to understand, and list the documents that would show it (reports, emails, contracts, maps, permits, meeting recordings).
   You'll know it worked when you have a who, an agency, a one-sentence aim, and a short list of record types.

2. **CV-FR-03-2.** Look up your state's law. Open `free/checklists/records-request-checklist.md` and its table. Search the web for your state's name plus "public records law," and choose the result on the state legislature's own site (or the attorney general's guide). Fill in every row, with a link and the date you checked. If a row stays blank, call the agency's records officer and ask.
   You'll know it worked when the table has an answer, a link, and a date in each row you need. If an AI model gave you a law or deadline you did not check on an official page, it's E08.

3. **CV-FR-03-3.** Open `free/prompts.md` and copy the "Rules block," then [prompt](../glossary.md#prompt) 5, Step B. Paste both into an AI model, fill in the brackets with what you verified, and send. Copy the draft into a Google Doc.
   You'll know it worked when the draft cites the law as you looked it up, has numbered items that each name a record type and a date range, asks for electronic copies and a fee estimate, and ends with a review checklist.

4. **CV-FR-03-4.** Review the draft against the review checklist. Change any item that is a question into a record ("records sufficient to show..."). Remove any request for private people's personal details. Make the tone neutral. Make the request one page if you can.
   You'll know it worked when every box on the checklist is ticked.

5. **CV-FR-03-5.** Send it by a method that leaves a dated record (the agency's online form, an email, or certified mail). Then add a row to the Records requests tab: the date sent, agency, subject, law cited, method, and the response due date counted from your lookup (write whether it is business or calendar days).
   You'll know it worked when you have a dated confirmation or a mailing receipt and a filled row.

6. **CV-FR-03-6.** Put the response due date and a follow-up date on your calendar. If you added the optional date highlighter ([CV-LC-02](CV-LC-02-add-the-date-highlighter.md)), it will color the date when it is close.
   You'll know it worked when the dates appear on your calendar.

7. **CV-FR-03-7.** If the date passes with no reply, send the polite follow-up from `skills/records-request/references/request-template.md`. If you get a fee estimate, a partial release, or a denial, reply with the matching template. A denial should name the [exemption](../glossary.md#exemption) used. Before any appeal, call your state's open-government help line or a legal aid group.
   You'll know it worked when you have a dated follow-up in the tracker. If the office never answers, it's E12.

8. **CV-FR-03-8.** When records arrive, save them unchanged in a dated folder under "Civic voice/Records requests." Write down, item by item, what you got and what you did not. Update the row's status.
   You'll know it worked when the folder and the row match what the office sent.

> **How to get help.** Write down the [step ID](../glossary.md#step-id) where you got stuck (it looks like CV-FR-01-3). If your kit has a [check-up](../glossary.md#check-up) button or command, run it and copy the [support code](../glossary.md#support-code) it prints. Then send both to Pigasus Group at https://pigasus.group/contact. Setup questions are free.
