# Tab layouts

The headers live in `tab-layouts.json`. One CSV per tab, named after the key (`monthly-traffic.csv`). Example rows use invented numbers.

| month | organic_sessions | organic_users | engaged_sessions | key_events | search_clicks | search_impressions | search_ctr | search_avg_position | data_through |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-09-01 | 1200 | 950 | 700 | 18 | 640 | 21000 | 0.030 | 14.2 | 2026-09-30 |

Notes: `month` is the first day of the month. CTR is a fraction. Use blank for "not available".
