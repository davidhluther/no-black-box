# Run the Monthly Cycle and Report to Leaders (MR Full, Guide 3)

The pulls exist and the owners are named. This guide is the monthly loop: check the numbers, write the report, gate it and put it in front of leaders in a form that holds up when someone asks "how do you know." About two hours of work, spread over the first week of the month. For the single-site version of the same loop, see [MM-FU-03](../../measure/setup/MM-FU-03-run-the-monthly-routine.md).

1. **MR-FU-03-1.** Confirm the [run log](../glossary.md#run-log) first. Open the run log and check that every scheduled pull for the month has an ok line, or a FAILED line followed by a retry. Note any gap. Re-run what you can by hand and record it.
   You'll know it worked when you can state, for each source, the first and last day of data you hold. Those become the [data-through dates](../glossary.md#data-through-date) in the report. If a pull is missing and cannot be rerun, it's E32 and the report names the gap instead of estimating.

2. **MR-FU-03-2.** Update the [change log](../glossary.md#change-log). Add every launch, edit, redesign, tracking change, campaign and outside [event](../glossary.md#event) for the month with its date, including what other teams shipped. If tracking changed, say which [events](../glossary.md#event) were added or renamed. New events come from `ga4-events-setup`, which builds an import file of new, prefixed pieces and never overwrites what runs today.
   You'll know it worked when each flagged move in step 4 can be lined up against a dated entry, or the log says nothing changed.

3. **MR-FU-03-3.** Pull the numbers into one table. Use the latest verified [Search Console](../glossary.md#search-console) capture, [Clarity](../glossary.md#clarity) totals, [GA4](../glossary.md#ga4) totals for the month and the month before, and your other sources from the [source map](../glossary.md#source-map). One row per measure, with this month, last month, the change, the source, the filter and the date range. Paid and organic are always labeled, and every search figure names its engine.
   You'll know it worked when no number in the table lacks a source or a date range. Leave a gap as "not available this month, because ..." rather than a zero.

4. **MR-FU-03-4.** Flag the moves and run `metric-check` on each one. Ask: "Use metric-check on every number that moved more than 20 percent with at least 100 events behind it." For each move it defines the measure, tests the measurement, lists the candidate causes, grades each one holds, weakened or refuted, and confirms direction on a second independent source. The causes it works through are your own changes, rankings and pages, AI answers, other teams' changes, outside forces, measurement and chance.
   You'll know it worked when every move has one block in the shape of `assets/flagged-move-template.md`, with a [grade](../glossary.md#grade) beside every cause. If two comparable sources disagree, the block says "[direction disputed](../glossary.md#direction-disputed)" and nothing recommends acting on it. If a move has only one platform behind it, it says "[single-source](../glossary.md#single-source)" and names the platform.

5. **MR-FU-03-5.** Apply the cross-platform rule on purpose, not by habit. For every finding, check it on every platform in the source map that measures it. Search direction should agree across Search Console, Bing and your SEO platform. [AI visibility](../glossary.md#ai-visibility) direction should agree across your AI-visibility platforms, your own question battery and GA4 AI referrals. Traffic and lead direction should agree across GA4, your [marketing automation](../glossary.md#marketing-automation) platform and your [CRM](../glossary.md#crm). Absolute sizes will differ, because prompt sets, competitor sets, engines, models and collection methods differ. Direction should not. When it does, find the reason in that list before anyone acts, and write the reason into the block.
   You'll know it worked when each block lists the sources that agreed. If a platform disagrees and you cannot explain it, the finding stays "direction disputed" and the explanation work goes on the owner's list, not into a "worth a look" line. If this keeps happening, it's E17.

6. **MR-FU-03-6.** Add the AI traffic view. Export the Search Console query list for the month and run the classifier from the `ai-traffic-classifier` [skill](../glossary.md#skill) folder: `python3 scripts/classify_queries.py queries.csv`. Report GA4 [AI referral traffic](../glossary.md#ai-referral-traffic) and the flagged query count side by side, each as a [floor](../glossary.md#floor), never as a total or a share. Read the flagged rows before quoting the count, and say plainly if nothing was flagged. Pull Clarity's AI channel with `fetch_clarity.py --ai-referrals` for a third view, labeled as a guess from the referring address.
   You'll know it worked when the report's AI section shows each count with its engine or tool, its dates and the words "at least". If a leader asks for "the AI share," the answer is that the data cannot give one, and the section says what it can give.

7. **MR-FU-03-7.** Write the report with `monthly-report`. Ask: "Use monthly-report for last month," and hand it the table, the `metric-check` blocks and the change log. The shape is a bottom line of three sentences, the numbers table, the flagged moves, what worked and what is broken, AI visibility as a floor, next month's actions with owners and dates, and caveats. Every finding ends in one action and one owner. Where someone else's decision drove a change, give the best case for it first, then judge it and name the test that settles it. Where money moved, give cost per result before and after.
   You'll know it worked when you have `monthly-report-<name>-<yyyy-mm>.md` and a [CSV](../glossary.md#csv) copy of the numbers table beside it. If a tracking or definition change sits inside the window, the report compares like with like or leaves the figure out and says why.

8. **MR-FU-03-8.** Gate it with `evidence-review` before it leaves the team. Ask: "Use evidence-review on this report." It grades each finding confirmed, likely, unverified or hypothesis, requires a named dated source for every number, tiers borrowed statistics from outside research, and tests every cause-and-effect claim for timing, size, other causes and mechanism. Also run `python3 scripts/check_report.py report.md` from the `metric-check` folder to catch a cause without a grade or a figure without a source. The sign-off person from the owner table runs the review, not the writer.
   You'll know it worked when the review returns a pass, or a fail with blockers you then fix and rerun. A hypothesis moves to an "Investigation needed" section and never into the action list.

9. **MR-FU-03-9.** Update the dashboard with `looker-export`. It writes one [CSV](../glossary.md#csv) per tab and runs `scripts/validate_tabs.py looker-export/` to check headers, dates and number formats. Append to the sheet behind your [Looker Studio](../glossary.md#looker-studio) dashboard; never replace it, because history is the point.
   You'll know it worked when the validator prints "ok" and the dashboard shows the new month. If Looker Studio says the data source has an error, it's E16.

10. **MR-FU-03-10.** Deliver it to leaders in a form they will read. Send the bottom line and the actions in the message itself, link the full report and the dashboard, and offer a 20-minute review for questions. Write the follow-up answers into the report, not into a thread, and state any correction outright ("earlier this said X; it is Y because Z"). Keep the last twelve months of reports in one folder, so a leader can see how the same figure was reported before.
    You'll know it worked when a leader can find the bottom line, the sources and the owner of each action without asking you. If a question comes back that the report did not answer, add the answer to the report.

11. **MR-FU-03-11.** Close the loop. At the start of next month, check last month's actions: done, in progress or dropped, each with an owner's name. Mark any prediction in last month's report as held or missed. Re-run `python3 support/doctor.py measurement-reporting` and keep the code with the report.
    You'll know it worked when next month's report opens by saying what happened to last month's actions.
