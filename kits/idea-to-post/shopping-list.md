# Shopping List

Every item below says what it is, why the kit needs it, what it costs, a cheaper or free option, whether you can skip it, and where you get to choose. Prices change. Where this page cannot show a price it checked today, it points you to the vendor's page instead. Every account or key is explained in the shared guide on [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md), including how to keep keys out of shared files and screenshots.

## Needed for every version

### A Google account

**What it is.** The login that gives you Google Sheets (spreadsheets), Docs (documents), and Drive (file storage).

**Why you need it.** The [queue](glossary.md#queue), the ideas list, the checklist, and the [check-up](glossary.md#check-up) all live there.

**Cost.** Free for a personal account. Eligible nonprofits can ask about Google Workspace for Nonprofits; see Google's current offer on its nonprofit page. Setup details are in [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md).

**Cheaper option.** It is already the free option.

**Skip it?** No. The free and low-cost versions are built on it. The full version can use plain folders instead, but you lose the shared sheet.

**Room to choose.** A personal account is fine. A shared organization account is better if more than one person works on posts.

### A way to talk to an AI model

**What it is.** A chat tool such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

**Why you need it.** It does the drafting, screening, and checking when you paste a [prompt](glossary.md#prompt).

**Cost.** ChatGPT has a free plan, and its pricing page confirms the free version is available to everyone (checked 11/13/2026); Claude has a free plan at $0 (checked 11/13/2026). Gemini's free access: see Google's current page. Account setup is covered in [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md).

**Cheaper option.** Use a free plan. Free plans limit how much you can send in a day and how long a pasted document can be.

**Skip it?** No.

**Room to choose.** Any of the three works with the prompts. Use the one you already have.

### Search Console

**What it is.** A free tool from Google that shows which searches bring people to your site. See [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md) for how to add your site.

**Why you need it.** It is the only source of your own search numbers, and it [feeds](glossary.md#feed) every measurement step.

**Cost.** See Google's current terms on the [Search Console](glossary.md#search-console) page; it has been offered at no charge, but this page did not verify that today.

**Cheaper option.** None needed.

**Skip it?** You can start without it, but you will be guessing when it is time to [refresh](glossary.md#refresh) a post. Add it early, because it only shows data from the day you verify your site.

**Room to choose.** Add the whole domain (best) or just one address.

### Access to your website editor

**What it is.** The login to the software behind your site (the [CMS](glossary.md#cms)), such as WordPress or Squarespace.

**Why you need it.** The kit prepares a post. A person places it.

**Cost.** Whatever you already pay for your site.

**Skip it?** No, unless someone else publishes for you. Then give that person the finished package.

## Useful in the free version

### Bing Webmaster Tools

**What it is.** Microsoft's version of Search Console. See [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md).

**Why.** A second view of your searches, including a [keyword](glossary.md#keyword) research view.

**Cost.** See Microsoft's current page; not verified today.

**Skip it?** Yes at first.

### Google Analytics 4 (GA4)

**What it is.** A free counter for visits and actions on your site, covered in [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md).

**Why.** It shows which posts lead to something useful, like a visit to your donation page.

**Cost.** See Google's current page; not verified today.

**Skip it?** Yes at first. If your site already has it, use it.

### Microsoft Clarity

**What it is.** A free tool that shows where visitors click and where they get stuck, without naming them. See [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md).

**Why.** It explains why a post that gets visits does not get action.

**Cost.** Free forever, according to its own site (checked 11/13/2026).

**Skip it?** Yes at first.

## Added in the low-cost version

### One paid AI plan

**What it is.** A monthly plan from ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini with higher limits and the ability to store your instructions and files in a [Project](glossary.md#project) or [Custom GPT](glossary.md#custom-gpt), so you do not paste them each time.

**Why.** It lets your rules stay put and lets you upload the kit's [skills](glossary.md#skill).

**Cost.** Claude Pro is $20 a month when billed monthly (checked 11/13/2026). For ChatGPT, see the current price at its pricing page, which did not show dollar amounts when this page checked on 11/13/2026. Gemini: see Google's current page. Account setup: [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md).

**Cheaper option.** Stay on the free plan and keep pasting the context block.

**Skip it?** Yes. Pay only once pasting your rules every time feels tedious.

**Room to choose.** Pick one. Do not pay for two.

### Apps Script

**What it is.** Google's tool for adding small programs to Sheets and Docs. It is part of your Google account and needs no separate sign-up.

**Why.** It powers the check-up button, the weekly feed pull, and the Search Console pull.

**Cost.** Included with your Google account. Google limits how much it runs per day; see Google's current page for the limits.

**Skip it?** Yes. The free version works without it, apart from the check-up button.

## Added in the full version

### Claude Code

**What it is.** A program from Anthropic that runs on your own computer. It reads and writes your files and runs this kit's tools. It is the program that runs the [AI model](glossary.md#ai-model) and its tools together (the official term is "[harness](glossary.md#harness)").

**Why.** It is what runs the skills and scripts with no pasting.

**Cost.** See the current plans at the vendor's pricing page; which plan includes it was not verified today. Account and key setup: [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md).

**Cheaper option.** The low-cost version.

**Skip it?** Yes, unless you publish often.

### Python

**What it is.** A programming language the kit's small scripts are written in.

**Why.** The idea fetcher, the topic screen, and the brief check are [Python](glossary.md#python) files.

**Cost.** Free from python.org.

**Skip it?** Only if you skip the scripts.

### A paid keyword tool (optional)

**What it is.** A service such as Ahrefs or Semrush that estimates how many people search for a phrase and how hard it is to rank.

**Why.** It speeds up research and fills the volume column in the [Queue](glossary.md#queue).

**Cost.** Paid. See the vendor's pricing page for current plans. If you buy one, set up the key as shown in [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md).

**Cheaper option.** Search Console and [Bing Webmaster Tools](glossary.md#bing-webmaster-tools), plus reading the first page of results yourself.

**Skip it?** Yes. Most small sites never need it.

### An image service (optional)

**What it is.** A design tool such as Canva, or a service that makes images from a written description.

**Why.** It produces banners and charts.

**Cost.** See each vendor's current pricing page; not verified today. Keys for image services are covered in [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md).

**Cheaper option.** Your own photos, free image libraries, and the designer brief the kit writes for you.

**Skip it?** Yes.

### Your own site's publishing connection (optional)

**What it is.** Permission for the publish step to talk to your website editor.

**Why.** It lets the practice run show exactly what would be created before a person confirms.

**Cost.** None beyond your site.

**Skip it?** Yes. Paste by hand instead.
