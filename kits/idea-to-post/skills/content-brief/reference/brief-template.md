# Brief template

New-post briefs use exactly this shape. Refresh briefs swap in the sections named in the content-brief skill. The first heading's prefix is how tools tell the two apart.

```markdown
# Content Brief: [Target search phrase]

## Keyword Data
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Target keyword | [phrase] |
| Monthly search volume | [number, or "not available" and why] |
| Keyword difficulty | [score, or "not available"] |
| Parent topic | [if different] |
| Features on the results page | [list] |
| Our site currently ranks? | [Yes or No, position] |

## Strategy Context
- Handoff note date: [date, or "none"]
- Stale or missing inputs: [list, or "none"]
- Why this topic now: [one line]

## Cross-Project Redundancy Check
| Where we looked | Result | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Live site (sitemap and site search) | | |
| Queue | | |
| Saved briefs and refresh notes | | |

**Recommended action:** [New post, Refresh (URL), Hold (resolve queue item), or Skip (already covered)]

## Voice of Customer
- Phrases from our own inbox and questions: [three to five, verbatim]
- Phrases from reviews or forums: [three to five, verbatim]
- Main pain to open with: [one or two sentences]

## Real User Questions
- [measured questions]

## Search Intent
[Definitional, Comparison, Implementation, or Cost and return, with the evidence]

## Target Audience
[who they are and what they care about]

## Core Value Proposition
[what we offer that is relevant, stated plainly]

## Competitive Landscape
| Who | Covers it? | Quality | Gap or opportunity |
|-----|-----------|---------|-------------------|

## What AI Answers Get Wrong
- Corrections to make:
- Formats that get cited:
- Criteria we should claim explicitly:

## Related Keywords and Questions
| Phrase or question | Volume | Difficulty | Use as |
|-----------------|--------|-----|--------|

## Verified Statistics
- [Statistic]: [Source], [Year], [Report title]   (at least eight, from at least five sources, none used more than twice)

## Internal Link Targets
- [natural phrase] to [URL]   (at least eight)

## Page for the Offer Section
- [program or service] to [URL]

## Article Outline
### Title: [holds the search phrase]
### Section heading, written as a question
[two or three sentences on what the section covers]
#### Sub-heading, as a question
### Section about our program or service (second to last)
### Frequently asked questions (last)

## Cost Report
| Call | Source | Cost |
|------|----------|------|
[Write "none, free sources only" if that is true.]
```
