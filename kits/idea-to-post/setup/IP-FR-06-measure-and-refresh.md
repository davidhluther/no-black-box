# IP-FR-06: Measure and Refresh

Search takes time. You will look at each post three times, at two, six, and 12 weeks, and decide whether to leave it, update it, or merge it. About 20 minutes per check.

1. **IP-FR-06-1.** Add three reminders in your calendar for each new post, two, six, and 12 weeks after it goes live. Put the post's title in each.
   You'll know it worked when the three reminders show in your calendar.

2. **IP-FR-06-2.** At a checkpoint, open [Search Console](../glossary.md#search-console), choose Performance, add a filter for the page's address, and set the date range to the time since it went live. Export the rows with the export button.
   You'll know it worked when you have a file or a copied table with clicks, impressions (how often it appeared), and average position (where it usually appears). If the page shows no data after four weeks, it's E10 or E21.

3. **IP-FR-06-3.** Paste the rows into [prompt](../glossary.md#prompt) 17 with the live date and the week number.
   You'll know it worked when you get a three-sentence summary in plain words and a list of searches that show you often but earn few clicks.

4. **IP-FR-06-4.** Add one row to the Measure tab with the page, date, checkpoint week, impressions, clicks, average position, top query, and a note.
   You'll know it worked when the Measure tab holds one row per checkpoint.

5. **IP-FR-06-5.** (Optional.) If you use Google Analytics 4, note whether visitors from search then visit your most useful page (open hours, donation, booking). If you use [Microsoft Clarity](../glossary.md#microsoft-clarity), watch a few recordings of that page for where visitors stall.
   You'll know it worked when you have a one-line note about what visitors did.

6. **IP-FR-06-6.** At the 12-week checkpoint, run prompt 18 with the old post and its numbers. It will recommend leaving it, refreshing it, or merging it into another post.
   You'll know it worked when you have a clear decision and, for a [refresh](../glossary.md#refresh), a list of what to keep, cut, and rewrite.

7. **IP-FR-06-7.** To refresh, repeat steps 4 through 9 of guide IP-FR-04 on the existing post. Change the visible date only when you changed something real, such as a new source or a rewritten section.
   You'll know it worked when the updated post is live and the [Queue](../glossary.md#queue) row notes the date and what changed.

Short on time or stuck? Go back to the [checklist](../free/post-checklist.md) and do only the next unchecked box.
