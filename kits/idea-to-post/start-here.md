# Idea to Live Post: Start Here

You get a repeatable path from "what should we write about?" to a post that is live on your website, checked for mistakes, illustrated, shared, and measured. You also get a Google Sheet that tracks every post, a checklist to follow for each one, and a set of [prompts](glossary.md#prompt) and tools that do the heavy lifting. The free version needs nothing you do not already have.

## Who this is for

Picture a hair salon with a blog nobody has time to write, or a small food bank that wants neighbors to find its open hours when they search. One person, often a volunteer or the owner, writes a post now and then. They are told they need an agency, an expensive search tool, or "a content strategy." Much of the industry around websites runs on that mystery. The method is not secret, and most of it fits on a checklist.

This kit opens the black box. It shows each step, why the step exists, and what to do without paying anyone.

## What you will have at the end

- A list of post ideas that comes from sources you picked, with a note on how much to trust each one.
- A screen that catches topics you already covered, or that nobody searches for, before you spend a day on them.
- A brief (a one- or two-page plan) for each post, with every statistic traced to a real source.
- A draft that follows your own voice and passes a plain-language check.
- Images with descriptions for people who cannot see them, and social posts that point to the article.
- A publish step where a person always makes the final click.
- Measurement at two, six, and 12 weeks, and a rule for when to update an old post.

## What it does not do

It does not promise rankings. Nobody honest can. Search results move for reasons outside your control, and a new post can take weeks to show up. It also does not publish for you in the free version, and it never publishes without a person confirming.

## The flow, step by step

| Step | What happens | [Skill](glossary.md#skill) or tool that does it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Find ideas from news and your readers' questions | industry-intel |
| 2 | Screen the ideas | topic-screen, content-gap |
| 3 | Research and write the brief | seo-research, synthetic-research, brief-layer, content-brief |
| 4 | Line up the work | content-handoff, draft-queue |
| 5 | Draft | seo-draft |
| 6 | Check quality | article-check, tell-scorer, copy-lint |
| 7 | Make images | hero-image, infographic-brief, visual-assets |
| 8 | Package for sharing | docx-export, linkedin-content, cta-inject |
| 9 | Publish | publish |
| 10 | Measure and decide what to update | gsc-read, clarity-analytics, ai-traffic-classifier, content-refresh |

A skill is a folder of instructions that teaches an [AI model](glossary.md#ai-model) one job. In the free version you do not install anything. You paste a prompt (the instructions you type to an AI model) from [free/prompts.md](free/prompts.md) instead.

## Fit it to your organization

Nothing here is a template to copy blindly. Your readers, your voice, and your website are specific. Every setting that is particular to you lives in one file, `config.example.json`, and the guides show you where each value goes. The prompts start with a short description of your organization so the AI model writes for your readers instead of for everyone.
