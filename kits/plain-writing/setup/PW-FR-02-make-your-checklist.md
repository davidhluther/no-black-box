# PW-FR-02: Make Your One-Page Checklist

You'll end with a one-page checklist in Google Docs that you can tick through before anything goes out. It takes about ten minutes.

1. **PW-FR-02-1.** Go to docs.google.com and sign in with your Google account.
   You'll know it worked when you see your list of documents, or a row of templates at the top. If a sign-in screen keeps coming back, it's E03.
2. **PW-FR-02-2.** Click Blank document (the white page with a plus sign).
   You'll know it worked when an empty page opens with a cursor blinking.
3. **PW-FR-02-3.** Open [checklist.md](../free/checklist.md) from this kit. Select everything from the second heading that reads "Plain Writing Checklist" (the one just under the horizontal line, followed by "Piece:") down to the end of the file, and copy it. Skip the title at the very top and the paragraph under it.
   You'll know it worked when the selection runs to the very end of the page.
4. **PW-FR-02-4.** Paste it into your blank Doc.
   You'll know it worked when you see all seven sections. Lines will show a dash and square brackets, like "- [ ]".
5. **PW-FR-02-5.** Remove the symbols. In the Doc, choose Edit, then Find and replace. In the Find box type these characters, with the spaces: `- [ ] ` (a dash, a space, a left square bracket, a space, a right square bracket, and a space after it). Leave the Replace box empty. Click Replace all, then close the box.
   You'll know it worked when the square brackets are gone and each line starts with its sentence.
6. **PW-FR-02-6.** Turn the lines into tick boxes. Select all the lines under section 1, then in the toolbar click the checklist icon (a box with a check). If you can't find it, open the Format menu, then Bullets & numbering, then Checklist. Repeat for the other six sections.
   You'll know it worked when each line has a small box you can click.
7. **PW-FR-02-7.** Title the Doc "Writing checklist, master." Then, each time you write something, choose File, Make a copy, and name the copy after the piece.
   You'll know it worked when you have a master and a copy side by side in Drive. Tick the copy and leave the master clean.

If a box feels unnecessary for your kind of writing, delete it. The checklist is yours.

The free version has no check-up button because nothing is installed, so also write what you see on the screen in a sentence or two, and take a screenshot if you can.
