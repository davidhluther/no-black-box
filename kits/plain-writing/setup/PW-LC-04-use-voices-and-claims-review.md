# PW-LC-04: Use the Voices and the Claims Review

You'll end able to ask your AI plan for a piece in one of three voices, and to check the claims in it before it goes out. It takes about 15 minutes.

This guide assumes you finished `PW-LC-01` ([skills](../glossary.md#skill)) or `PW-LC-02` (a [Project](../glossary.md#project)) with the voice files loaded.

1. **PW-LC-04-1.** Pick a voice. Quick Take is short and skeptical, for posts. Analyst Memo is a rigorous memo that moves from problem to mechanism to recommendation. Straight Talk is a candid critique that names who would have to give something up. Read the example at the bottom of each file in [voice-presets](../skills/voice-presets/SKILL.md) to hear the difference.
   You'll know it worked when you can say which one fits your next piece.
2. **PW-LC-04-2.** Ask for it by name, with your material. For example: "Write this up in the Analyst Memo voice for our board. Here are my notes," followed by your notes.
   You'll know it worked when the reply opens on the problem as it first looks, explains the mechanism, and ends on something specific, not on a recap.
3. **PW-LC-04-3.** Run the claims review before anything goes to a board, funder, or client. Ask: "Use the evidence-review skill on this draft" (or paste [Prompt](../glossary.md#prompt) 4 from [prompts.md](../free/prompts.md) if you didn't [upload](../glossary.md#upload) the skill).
   You'll know it worked when you get each claim with a grade (Confirmed, Likely, Unverified, or Hypothesis) and a list of numbers you must check yourself.
4. **PW-LC-04-4.** Check each number against its source. Open the original, not an article about it. Write the date you looked next to the number.
   You'll know it worked when every figure in the piece has a source you opened. Delete any you couldn't verify, or say it in words.
5. **PW-LC-04-5.** Have one person who didn't write it read the final piece, and ask them to try to disprove the three claims that matter most.
   You'll know it worked when they've found nothing, or you've fixed what they found.
