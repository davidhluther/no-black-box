# PW-FR-04: Test It on the Example Draft

You'll end knowing the free setup works, because you'll watch it catch the problems we planted in a sample draft. It takes about 15 minutes.

1. **PW-FR-04-1.** Open [example-draft.md](../free/example-draft.md) and copy all of it.
   You'll know it worked when your clipboard holds a short piece about a food pantry's volunteer sign-up sheet. Every number in it is invented.
2. **PW-FR-04-2.** Start a new chat and paste [Prompt](../glossary.md#prompt) 1 (the rules prompt) from PW-FR-01, with your [house style](../glossary.md#house-style) if you have one. Send it.
   You'll know it worked when the [AI model](../glossary.md#ai-model) replies that it understands.
3. **PW-FR-04-3.** In the same chat, paste Prompt 2 (the review prompt), then paste the example draft under the word DRAFT, and send. 
   You'll know it worked when you get a table of findings. It should flag at least these: "In today's ever-evolving world," "leverage," "Moreover," "robust," "testament," "seamless," "It's important to note," "In conclusion," more than one "not just" or "isn't X, it's Y" shape, and the [title-case](../glossary.md#title-case) heading "What We Changed." If it catches most of them, you're set. If it misses many, or gives praise instead of a table, it's E06. Paste Prompt 1 again and retry.
4. **PW-FR-04-4.** Now run Prompt 4 (the claims prompt) on the same draft.
   You'll know it worked when it lists the figures in the draft (42 people, 30 slots, 11 no-shows) and says it can't check them. Because the numbers are invented, that's the right answer. If it confirms a number as true or invents a source, it's E23.
5. **PW-FR-04-5.** Open [example-draft-fixed.md](../free/example-draft-fixed.md) and read it beside the original.
   You'll know it worked when you can see what changed. The fixed version names a real person, takes out the stage-setting, ends on an open question, and says what the writer doesn't know.

You can now use the free version on your own writing. Paste your draft in place of the example.

> **How to get help.** Write down the [step ID](../glossary.md#step-id) where you got stuck (it looks like PW-FR-01-3). The free version has no check-up button because nothing is installed, so also write what you see on the screen in a sentence or two, and take a screenshot if you can. Then send both to Pigasus Group at https://pigasus.group/contact. Setup questions are free.
