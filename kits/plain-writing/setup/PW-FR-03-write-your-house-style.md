# PW-FR-03: Write Your House Style

You'll end with a short document that holds the writing rules particular to your organization. Three lines is enough to start. It takes about 15 minutes.

A house style (a short set of your own rules, such as how you spell your name and what you never say) sits on top of the shared rules. It can make them stricter. It can't make them looser.

1. **PW-FR-03-1.** Open [house-style-template.md](../skills/plain-writing/references/house-style-template.md) and [house-style-example.md](../skills/plain-writing/assets/house-style-example.md) in two browser tabs.
   You'll know it worked when you can see a blank template in one tab and a filled one for an invented food pantry in the other.
2. **PW-FR-03-2.** Open a new Google Doc and title it "House style." Copy the template into it.
   You'll know it worked when the Doc shows the headings Who we are, Who reads this, Voice, Numbers and dates, Extra rules, Checks before anything goes out, and Where this came from.
3. **PW-FR-03-3.** Fill in the three parts that matter most. Under Who we are, write how your name appears in prose. Under Who reads this, write one sentence about your main reader. Under Voice, write three words for how you sound. Delete every heading you can't fill in yet.
   You'll know it worked when your Doc is under one page and every line is something you actually believe.
4. **PW-FR-03-4.** Add one extra rule that comes from a real mistake: a word that bothered a reader, a name that gets misspelled, a fact that was once wrong. Write one line saying what went wrong. If a rule from the kit fires on something that's correct, such as a quote or a product name, add that exception here too, so the next person knows why (see E24 in [fixes](../fixes.md)).
   You'll know it worked when your rule has a reason written next to it.
5. **PW-FR-03-5.** Make the AI chat use it. Open your rules [prompt](../glossary.md#prompt) Doc from PW-FR-01 and paste your house style under the line that says HOUSE STYLE. Do the same in Prompt 2.
   You'll know it worked when you ask the chat, "What does my house style say about our name?" and it answers correctly.

Keep it short. Every rule should come from something that went wrong once.
