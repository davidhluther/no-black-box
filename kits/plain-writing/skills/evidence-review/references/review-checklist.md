# Evidence Review Checklist

One page to run by hand on any document before it leaves the building.

1. List every claim that says something is wrong, says one thing caused another, or recommends spending money or time.
2. For each, name the primary source and its date. If you can only name the last document that said it, the claim is unsourced.
3. Grade it: Confirmed, Likely, Unverified, Hypothesis, or Refuted.
4. For every cause-and-effect claim, check timing, size, other causes, and mechanism.
5. For every borrowed statistic, read the original and give it a tier from A to D. Tier D comes out.
6. Check that each number carries its observation date and belongs to the exact page, program, or period named.
7. Look for site, tool, or tracking changes that could fake a trend.
8. Compare against your known-bad claims list, by meaning and not only by number.
9. Ask someone who did not write it to try to disprove the three claims that matter most.
10. Write the verdict: pass, or fail with the blockers listed.
