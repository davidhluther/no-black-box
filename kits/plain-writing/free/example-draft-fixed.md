# How We Cut Our Volunteer Sign-Up Time in Half

Our sign-up sheet had one real problem. Nobody owned it. Forty-two people signed up for 30 shifts in March (invented numbers, for the example), eleven showed up on the wrong day, and a neighbor named Dana ended up covering the door alone on a Saturday.

## Why the old sheet failed

Volunteers, board members, and staff all edited the same tab. Nobody could tell which row was current, and when two people changed the same row, the last edit won without a word.

## What we changed

We made one tab per month, and each tab has one owner. The owner's name sits in cell A1, so anyone with a question knows who to ask. We also moved reminders to a free text-message tool that sends a note two days before each shift.

The next month, nobody showed up on the wrong day. We still don't know whether the owner or the reminder did the work, and we haven't tried one without the other.
