# How We Cut Our Volunteer Sign-Up Time in Half

In today's ever-evolving world, a food pantry has to leverage every volunteer hour. Moreover, our robust sign-up sheet was a testament to how seamless a spreadsheet can be. It's important to note that this is an example draft, written on purpose with the habits the checker catches.

## Why the old sheet failed

The problem isn't the spreadsheet. It's the way we used it. Our team of volunteers, board members, and staff all edited the same tab, and nobody knew which row was current. Not just that, but the sheet had no owner.

Here is what happened in March, using invented numbers. Forty-two people signed up for 30 slots. Eleven showed up on the wrong day. The pantry ran short on a Saturday, and a neighbor named Dana covered the door alone.

## What We Changed

We made one tab per month. Each tab has one owner, and the owner's name sits in cell A1. We also moved the reminders to a free text-message tool, which now sends a note two days before each shift.

In conclusion, a simple owner and a simple reminder fixed more than any new software could.
