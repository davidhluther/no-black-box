# Template: year-end donor-advised fund reminder

When to send. Mid-November, to donors known or likely to hold a donor-advised fund (a charitable account a donor holds at a sponsor, from which the donor recommends gifts). It is also a useful message for the whole list. Fill the bracketed parts.

Subject: A simple way to give before year-end

Dear [First name],

As the year winds down, many of our friends give through a donor-advised fund, the charitable account they have already set aside for exactly this purpose. If you have one at a brokerage's charity arm, a community foundation or another sponsor, recommending a grant to [Organization name] takes just a few minutes.

Your fund administrator will need only this:

- Organization: [Legal name]
- Tax ID: [00-0000000]
- Address: [Mailing address]

You can also start a grant from our website at [web address of your fund-gift page].

[One true sentence about this year's work, using a number from your claims register (a table of every fact you may state about your organization, each with its source).] A grant from your fund goes straight to that work.

Thank you for being part of this. If you or your fund administrator have questions, reply to this email.

With gratitude,
[Name]
[Title], [Organization name]

This message is general information, not tax advice.
