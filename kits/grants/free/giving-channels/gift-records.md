# Recording gifts by channel

Mixing the four kinds of record below is how a gift report ends up counting money that has not arrived. This page covers gift tracking only. When a gift counts as revenue in your books follows your finance policy and your accountant.

## Keep four kinds apart

| Kind | Example | Counted as a received gift? | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|---|
| Confirmed online payment | A $50 card gift on the donation form | Yes, once the payment is confirmed (it may not have reached the bank yet) | The gift record the form creates, with the charge date |
| Pending transfer | A notice that 40 shares are on the way | No, not until it arrives | A note on the donor record, tagged with the channel's code |
| Bequest (a gift left in someone's will) intention | "I've included you in my will" | No | A note on the donor record, tagged BEQUEST, private if the donor asked |
| Estate gift received | A check from an executor | Yes, on the date received | A gift record tagged BEQUEST |

## Channel codes

Use one short code per channel so one saved view or one filter totals each: DAF, STOCK, QCD (a gift sent straight from an IRA to a charity), BEQUEST, MONTHLY. Online forms can preset the code. Gifts that arrive by check or transfer are tagged when entered.

## Channel by channel

| Channel | When the money shows up | How to enter it | How to thank the donor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Online, once or monthly | Right away | The form creates the gift | Automatic receipt. Changes to a monthly gift go through the development contact |
| Donor-advised fund (a charitable account a donor holds at a sponsor, from which the donor recommends gifts) grant | Days to weeks after the donor recommends it | Gift from the sponsor's record, soft credit to the donor, code DAF | Thank-you letter to the donor within a week. No tax-receipt wording, because the sponsor already gave the donor one |
| Stock | When the shares land in your account | Gift dated the day shares arrived, transfer record kept, code STOCK | Letter naming the security, number of shares and date received, with the "no goods or services" statement. You describe the shares; you do not state their value for the donor |
| IRA (QCD) | When the custodian's check arrives | Gift from the IRA owner, check date and date received recorded, code QCD | Letter with amount, date received and the "no goods or services" statement, noting it came from the donor's IRA |
| Bequest intention | Never, as a gift | Note on the donor record | Personal call or letter from the development lead |
| Estate gift received | When the estate pays | Gift, code BEQUEST | Letter to the executor, and to family if they want one |

## Gifts that arrive without a name

DAF grants, stock transfers and IRA checks often show only the sponsor's, broker's or custodian's name. Check gift notices and the development inbox first. If nothing matches, finance holds the gift under the sender's name and development follows up with the sender.

## Year-end

Record the facts and keep the paper trail: the charge date for card gifts, the postmark and date received for checks, the date shares arrived with the broker's transfer record, and the custodian's check date and date received for IRA gifts. Do not tell donors which tax year a gift falls in. The IRS rules differ by method (see IRS Publication 526, "When To Deduct"), so donors confirm timing with their broker, custodian or tax advisor. That is why pages ask donors to start early.
