# Funder Research Checklist

Copy this into a Google Doc (File, then Make a copy of a page you paste into, or just paste it) and tick the boxes for each funder. One checklist per funder.

Funder: ______________________  Date started (mm/dd/yyyy): ____________

## Before you call them a prospect

- [ ] Searched our tracker's Pipeline tab for the name and spelling variants
- [ ] Searched past grant reports and board minutes for the name
- [ ] Searched bank deposit records for the name (spellings vary)
- [ ] Asked the person who handled grants before us
- [ ] Wrote down where I looked in the "Where we checked" column of the Funders tab

If they are a current or past funder, they are not a prospect. Change the Status and stop here.

## What to find out

- [ ] Funder's own website opened by me, not copied from a search result summary
- [ ] Giving priorities copied in their words, with the web address and date
- [ ] How to apply (open, letter of inquiry (a short first letter some funders ask for), invitation only, no unsolicited)
- [ ] Deadline or yearly cycle
- [ ] Award range, and what they actually gave nearby (tax filing on ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer (a free website that shows nonprofit tax filings))
- [ ] Geography rule (where they will and will not give)
- [ ] Rules that might exclude us (tax status, budget cap, statement of belief)
- [ ] Names of officers from the tax filing (public record only)

## Mark the confidence

- [ ] Each fact is marked verified, secondary or unverified in the Funders tab
- [ ] Every web address was opened by me and works

## Decide the tier and the next step

- [ ] Tier 1, 2 or 3 chosen (see the Rubric tab)
- [ ] Suggested ask written, with the reason
- [ ] One next step written, with a date
- [ ] Nothing was sent to the funder. Contact happens only after the board lead says yes.

## Do not

- Do not drop a funder only because the evidence is thin. Give it a lower tier and a note.
- Do not try to get past a "prove you are human" test on any website. Slow down or look again tomorrow.
- Do not create an account on a funder's portal for research.
