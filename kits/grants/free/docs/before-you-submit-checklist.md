# Before You Submit Checklist

Use this on every draft, after the DraftCheck tab shows no problems. A person other than the writer should do the review section.

Funder: ______________________  Draft: ______________________  Deadline (mm/dd/yyyy): ____________

## Gate

- [ ] This funder is not a current funder asking for something other than a renewal
- [ ] The funder does not require a statement or eligibility we cannot honestly give
- [ ] The deadline has not passed, and the channel is open to us
- [ ] No one else on staff is already drafting this application

## Facts

- [ ] Every number in the draft is in the Claims tab with a status of source-verified or external-verified, or is on the allowed list with a reason
- [ ] Each number appears once, with the period it covers
- [ ] No place we do not serve is named as served
- [ ] No client names, dates of birth or case notes anywhere
- [ ] Every [CONFIRM] note has been answered by the right person, or removed

## Fit

- [ ] I quoted the funder's current guidelines, copied today from their own site
- [ ] The draft answers every question they ask, in their order
- [ ] A table lists each requirement, where the draft answers it, and whether the attachment is ready
- [ ] The ask matches their published range and what they give nearby

## Independent review

- [ ] A second person (or a fresh AI chat using the fresh-chat review message in `prompts.md`) looked for missed requirements and unsupported claims
- [ ] Problems found are fixed or listed in the cover note

## Approval and sending

- [ ] The right person approved this draft (see the tier approvals page)
- [ ] The person who will send it is the person the board chose
- [ ] Date sent and a copy saved: ____________
- [ ] Pipeline row updated (Status, Submitted date, Deadline, Next step)
