# Tier Approvals: One Page for the Board

Nothing goes out in the organization's name until the board approves this page. The board chooses who sends.

| Tier | What it covers | Who approves each ask | Who sends |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Large asks and relationship asks. Request at or above the Tier 1 minimum in the tracker's Settings tab. | The board, case by case | [name chosen by the board] |
| Tier 2 | Mid-size open calls with a real fit. Request between the Tier 2 and Tier 1 minimums. | One batch approval from the grants lead or board chair, listing the funders | [name chosen by the board] |
| Tier 3 | Short forms and letter-of-inquiry emails under the Tier 2 minimum. | One standing approval for the whole tier, renewed every [six months] | [name chosen by the board] |

## Ground rules

- A board member who holds a public seat or a business tie to a funder does not carry that ask. Another member does.
- Drafts use only claims from the Claims tab. Numbers that are not there wait for a person to confirm them.
- Where the tracker disagrees with a grant letter, the books or the minutes, the source document wins.
- No one creates accounts or types passwords on a funder's portal without the board lead's yes.
