# Giving Channels Checklist

One line per way of giving. A person with access to your website and bank or brokerage must do the steps marked with a person sign.

## Donor-advised funds

- [ ] Page drafted from the template, with legal name, EIN (the nine-digit federal tax ID), mailing address and a contact
- [ ] (person) Optional free fund widget registered and code pasted in
- [ ] (person) Page published and linked from the Donate menu

## Employer matching gifts

- [ ] Plain-text message added to the donation form
- [ ] Plain-text message added to the confirmation page
- [ ] Follow-up email ready
- [ ] Decision noted: free text for now, or a search tool later (decide only after seeing how many donors ask)

## Monthly giving

- [ ] (person) Recurring form built in the donation platform, monthly pre-selected
- [ ] Amounts tied to true, current costs and checked against the budget
- [ ] (person) Form linked prominently, not buried under "other ways to give"

## Stock and IRA gifts

- [ ] (person) Brokerage account that can receive securities confirmed, details written down by the treasurer
- [ ] Page drafted with firm, transfer number and account number (never in an email thread)
- [ ] "A gift is on its way" form drafted, so staff can match unnamed transfers
- [ ] IRA gift letter ready, with the tax disclaimer

## Gifts in a will

- [ ] Exact legal name and EIN confirmed for the sample language
- [ ] Page drafted with development contact
- [ ] List of five to ten loyal, long-time donors chosen for a personal note
- [ ] Soft-ask letter personalized for each. No mass mailing.

## Round-up and rewards

- [ ] Grocery community-rewards enrollments done
- [ ] Local utility or retail round-up programs asked

## Recording

- [ ] Channel codes chosen (DAF, STOCK, QCD (a gift sent straight from an IRA to a charity), BEQUEST (a gift left in someone's will), MONTHLY) and added to the donor database or sheet
- [ ] Thank-you wording approved for each kind of gift
- [ ] Quarterly board table scheduled
