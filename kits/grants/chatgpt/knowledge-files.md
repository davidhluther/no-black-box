# Knowledge Files for the ChatGPT or Claude Project

Upload these files to your Project's knowledge (the files the AI model (a program that reads and writes text) can read when it answers). A ChatGPT Project or Custom GPT can hold up to 20 files, so this list stops at 17 and leaves room for three more. File paths are inside the kit's folder, `kits/grants`, in the folder you downloaded.

## Method files, upload once

1. `glossary.md`
2. `free/tracker/Rubric.csv`
3. `free/prompts.md`
4. `free/docs/funder-research-checklist.md`
5. `free/docs/before-you-submit-checklist.md`
6. `free/docs/tier-approvals.md`
7. `skills/funder-research/SKILL.md`
8. `skills/funder-research/references/dossier-template.md`
9. `skills/grant-drafting/SKILL.md`
10. `skills/grant-drafting/references/procedure.md`
11. `skills/grant-drafting/references/claims-register.md`
12. `skills/giving-channels/SKILL.md`
13. `skills/giving-channels/references/channels.md`
14. `skills/giving-channels/references/gift-records.md`

## Your own files, refresh when they change

15. Your Claims tab. In Google Sheets (Google's free spreadsheet app), open the tab, then File, Download, Comma-separated values. Upload that file and name it `claims.csv`.
16. Your Funders tab, downloaded the same way and named `funders.csv`.
17. Your Pipeline tab, downloaded the same way and named `pipeline.csv`.

Never upload donor lists, client records, passwords or bank details. Your Claims, Funders and Pipeline tabs hold funder names and organization facts only.

## Upload when you need them

The letter and page templates (`free/giving-channels/`: the email for gifts from a donor-advised fund (a charitable account a donor holds at a sponsor), monthly appeal, IRA letter, will letter and matching-gift copy). Upload only the one you are working on, then replace it with the next.

## Paste the instructions

Open `chatgpt/instructions.md`, copy everything, and paste it into the Project's instructions box (or the Custom GPT's instructions box).
