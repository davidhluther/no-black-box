## How the pieces fit

| Piece | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Wider Net tab | Funding sources beyond existing relationships, sorted by roadmap stage |
| Pipeline tab | Every ask on record plus the next prospects, with status, amounts and fit score |
| Funder Intel tab | One verdict per funding stream |
| Funder Dossier Index tab | Foundations from public tax filings, sorted into tiers |
| Dashboard tab | Win rate, dollars awarded, open asks and tier mix |
| Funder dossiers | Research files on the top prospects |
| Research files | The source reports behind the Wider Net tab, with each claim marked verified, secondary or unverified |

## Ground rules

Conflicts of interest and who carries which ask. The research is not the record: where it disagrees with a grant letter, the books or the minutes, the source document wins. Note which dates are inferred from past cycles.
