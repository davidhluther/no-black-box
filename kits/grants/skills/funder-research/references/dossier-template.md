# Funder dossier template

File name: `<slug>.md`, the funder's name in lowercase with hyphens (for example `example-family-foundation.md`).

```markdown
---
funder: <legal name>
ein: <EIN, nine digits>
slug: <slug>
city_state: <City, ST>
type: private foundation | community foundation | corporate | government | public charity
assets_usd: <latest from the 990-PF, with the year>
our_relationship: none found | past funder | current funder | declined previously   # say where you checked
priority_tier: A | B | C
status: prospect | researching | cultivating | applied | awarded | declined | parked
last_researched: YYYY-MM-DD
---

## Snapshot
Two or three sentences a grants lead reads first: who they are, whether you fit, and the one thing to do next.

## Giving priorities (in their words)
Quote or closely paraphrase the published guidelines. Link each one.

## How to apply
Open application, letter of inquiry, invitation only, or no unsolicited proposals. Portal or email, deadlines or cycles, award range, restrictions, required attachments. Date verified.

## Grants in our area
From the 990-PF, Candid or ProPublica: grantee, city, amount, purpose, year. Note which are in your field.

## People and connections
Trustees and officers from the 990-PF, program staff, and any known link to your board, staff or donors. Public-record names only.

## Fit and tailored angle
Which program to pitch, which claims-register facts to lead with (cite their IDs), the language to mirror from their guidelines, and a suggested ask with the reasoning.

## Open questions for staff
What a person must confirm before any ask.

## Sources
- <URL> (verified | secondary, checked YYYY-MM-DD)

## Log
- YYYY-MM-DD: what was learned or done, and by whom.
```

Keep an `INDEX.md` with one row per dossier (funder, tier, status, next deadline) so the list can be sorted.
