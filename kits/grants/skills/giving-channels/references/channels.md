# Channel by channel setup

Owners and times are typical for a small team. Adjust to your own staff.

## 1. Donor-advised funds

- What to set up. A page titled something like "Give from a donor-advised fund" with your legal name, EIN, mailing address, and a contact for questions. Optionally add a free embeddable widget from a DAF sponsor (read its terms at its own site first, and register your EIN there).
- Person steps. Register the organization with any widget you use, paste its code into the page, publish, and link the page from the main Donate menu.
- Time. About an hour. Owner: development, with whoever edits the website.
- Why first. It costs nothing and opens a channel that larger and more rating-conscious donors already use.

## 2. Employer matching gifts

- Free version, start today. Place a short plain-text prompt on the donation form and confirmation page (see `templates/matching-gifts.md`). Add a follow-up email.
- Paid version, later. Search tools put a company-search box on the form and tell the donor whether their employer matches. Some donation platforms bundle a lighter version, so check yours before buying.
- Time. A few hours for the free version. Owner: development.

## 3. Monthly giving

- What to set up. A recurring-gift form in the donation platform you already use, with monthly pre-selected and three amounts. Tie each amount to a real, current cost ("$15 a month covers a week of meals") and check those lines against your budget before publishing.
- Name the monthly club only if you will keep writing to its members each season.
- Time. About a day. Why it matters. Recurring donors tend to give more over time and make cash flow predictable.

## 4. Stock and IRA gifts

- Person steps. Confirm the organization has a brokerage account that can receive securities (open one if not; this is a treasurer task). Put the brokerage firm, its transfer number and the account number on a page or a printable sheet, and add a "tell us a gift is coming" form so staff can match unnamed transfers.
- IRA gifts. Link to your donors' custodians' own online request pages where they exist, and offer a one-page letter donors can sign for any other custodian. Remind donors that the tax details are theirs to confirm with an advisor.
- Time. The page is quick. The brokerage account is the gating item.
- Why. Donor bases and boards often skew older, so IRA gifts fit, and stock gifts tend to be larger than cash.

## 5. Gifts in a will

- Person steps. Add the development contact to the page, publish, and begin quietly asking your most loyal, longest-tenured donors, using `templates/legacy-soft-ask.md`.
- Sample language needs the organization's exact legal name and EIN. Confirm them first, and tell donors to take the wording to their own attorney. Other ways to leave a gift, such as naming the organization as a retirement-account beneficiary, can sit on the same page.
- Never pressure. The goal is to let people say "I've included you."

## 6. Round-up and rewards

Enroll in grocery community-rewards cards and any local utility or retail round-up programs. Each is an enrollment, not a build. Owner: development.

## Honest framing

These channels widen the donor base and steady cash. None closes a structural budget gap alone.
