# Template: gift in a will, a soft ask

Use as a letter, or as a script for the executive director or a board member to use with your most loyal, longest-tenured donors. The goal is to open a conversation and let people say "I've included you." Personalize it heavily. It should never read like a mass mailing.

## Letter

Subject: Something I've been meaning to ask you

Dear [First name],

You have stood with [Organization name] for [number] years now, and I think of you as part of the family here. That is why I wanted to raise something quietly, with no expectation attached.

Some of our closest friends have chosen to remember [Organization name] in their wills. It is a way of keeping the door open long after today, and it costs nothing now. It can be as simple as one sentence in an estate plan.

I'm not asking for a decision, or for a conversation you are not ready for. I wanted you to know the option exists, and that if you have already included us, or are thinking about it, I would like to thank you and hear what you would want your gift to support.

If you would like to talk it through, I am glad to, with no obligation.

With gratitude,
[Name]
[Title], [Organization name]

## If they show interest

- Share the sample language below and suggest they take it to their own attorney.
- Offer to note their intention for internal records. Never require documentation.
- Offer recognition only in ways they are comfortable with. Many prefer to stay anonymous.

## Sample language (confirm your exact legal name and EIN first)

"I give to [Legal name] (EIN [00-0000000]), a nonprofit organization located in [City, State], [the sum of $____ / ____ percent of my estate / the remainder of my estate] to be used for its general charitable purposes."

This is a sample for discussion with the donor's attorney, not legal advice.
