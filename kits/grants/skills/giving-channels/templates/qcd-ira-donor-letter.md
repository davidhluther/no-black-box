# Template: IRA gift (qualified charitable distribution) letter

When to send. October, to donors likely to be 70½ or older, such as longtime supporters. Fill the bracketed parts. Keep the disclaimer.

Subject: A tax-smart way to give from your IRA

Dear [First name],

I wanted to make sure you know about a way of giving that many longtime friends find especially useful.

If you are 70½ or older, you can give directly from your IRA to [Organization name], up to the annual limit set by the IRS, and the gift is not counted as taxable income for you. If you are required to take a minimum distribution this year, a gift like this can count toward it.

Setting it up is simple. Ask your IRA administrator to send a qualified charitable distribution to:

- [Legal name]
- Tax ID (EIN): [00-0000000]
- Address: [Mailing address]

Please let us know if you make a gift this way. These gifts sometimes arrive with no name attached, and we want to thank you properly.

With appreciation,
[Name]
[Title], [Organization name]

This letter is general information, not tax advice. Please confirm the details and the current limit with your own financial or tax advisor.
