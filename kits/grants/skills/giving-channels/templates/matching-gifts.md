# Template: employer matching gift copy

Many donors work for companies that will match their gift, and most never claim it. Use the copy below on the donation form, the confirmation page and in a follow-up email. It works with or without a paid search tool.

## On the donation form

Double your gift at no extra cost. Many companies match their employees' charitable gifts, some at one to one. It usually takes about five minutes. Search your company's HR or giving site for "matching gifts," or ask your HR office. Send completed match forms to [giving contact email] and we will handle the rest.

## On the confirmation page

Thank you. Now make it go further. Does your employer match charitable gifts? If so, your gift could be worth twice as much. Email [giving contact email] and we will walk you through it.

## Follow-up email

Subject: Your gift could be worth double

Thank you again for your recent gift. Many employers match donations from their staff, which would double your impact at no cost to you. It usually takes a few minutes through your company's giving portal. If you are not sure whether your employer matches, reply to this email and we will help you find out.
