# Shopping List

Everything you might need, in the order you would need it. For each item you will find what it is, why the kit uses it, what it costs, what to use instead, whether you can skip it, and where you have room to choose. Costs say when they were checked. Where a price could not be confirmed on the vendor's own page, the entry says to see the current price there. Sign-up help for every account and key is in the shared [keys and accounts guide](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md).

## Needed for the free version

| Item | What it is and why you need it | Cost | Instead, or can you skip it? | Room to choose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A Google account | Your sign-in to Google's apps. The tracker lives in Google Sheets (Google's free spreadsheet app), checklists live in Google Docs (its free word processor), and files live in Google Drive (its online storage). Sign-up help is in the [keys and accounts guide](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md). | Free for any personal account. | You cannot skip it. A Microsoft Excel user can import the same CSV files (plain tables saved as text), but the check-up (the Help menu button that tests your sheet's setup) and the draft checker need Google Sheets. | Use an organization account if you have one, so the tracker doesn't live in one volunteer's personal account. |
| Google Workspace for Nonprofits (Google's free offer of its office apps to eligible nonprofits) | It gives your organization its own accounts, such as grants@example.org, and shared storage. See the [keys and accounts guide](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md). | The base plan was listed at $0 per user per month (checked 11/13/2026 at https://www.google.com/nonprofits/offerings/workspace/). Google verifies eligibility first, and paid tiers are discounted, not free. | Skip it and use a plain Google account. You lose only the organization-owned accounts. | Apply early, because verification takes time. Use whichever account the board is comfortable owning. |
| A free AI model (a program that reads and writes text when you message it, such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini) | ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, in its free form. The prompts (messages you paste in) in this kit work in any of them. | Free tiers exist. Limits change, so see each vendor's own page: https://chatgpt.com/pricing, https://claude.com/pricing and Google's Gemini page. | Skip it and follow the checklists by hand. You lose speed on reading funder pages and drafting. | Pick the one you already use. If a free tier runs out mid-task, wait a day or try another. |
| ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer (a free website that shows nonprofit tax filings) | A free website that shows the IRS tax filings of nearly every US nonprofit, including what a foundation paid out. See [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md); no account is needed. | Free, with no sign-in needed to browse and search. Details are at https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/. | Candid's free profiles show some of the same data. You cannot skip tax filings, because they show what funders really give. | Use either site, or both when they disagree. |
| The kit's files | The CSV files, prompts, checklists and two scripts in the `free` folder. They are plain text. They live on GitHub (the website where these files live) at https://github.com/davidhluther/no-black-box. The [get the kit files](../../guides/get-the-files.md) guide shows how to download them. | Free. A GitHub account is not needed to download. | Cannot skip. | None. |

## Added in the low-cost version

| Item | What it is and why you need it | Cost | Instead, or can you skip it? | Room to choose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| One AI plan | A paid plan of ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, about $20 a month, which lets you keep your kit documents in a Project (a saved workspace that remembers your files and instructions). | Claude Pro was listed at $20 a month billed monthly, or $17 a month billed annually at $200 up front (checked 11/13/2026 at https://claude.com/pricing). For ChatGPT, see the current price at https://chatgpt.com/pricing. For Gemini, see the current price on Google's Gemini plans page. | You can skip it and stay on the free version. You then paste your documents into each chat by hand. | Any one of the three. Don't pay for more than one. Cancel any time. |
| Apps Script (a free Google tool that runs small programs inside a spreadsheet) | The free tool inside Google Sheets that lets you paste in a small program, called a script, to add a menu or a weekly job. See [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md). | Free with a Google account. Google limits how much a script can do each day; see https://developers.google.com/apps-script/guides/services/quotas. A weekly search stays far inside the limits. | You can skip the weekly search script and run the search by hand. The check-up button also needs Apps Script. | None; there is one way to do this. |
| Grants.gov (the US government's public list of grant opportunities) | The US government's public grant listing. The weekly search reads from it. See [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md). | Free. The search the script uses is a public one that needs no key. Details are at https://www.grants.gov/. | Skip it if federal grants do not fit your work. State and local funders are separate. | Change the search words in `sweep.gs`. |

## Added in the full version

| Item | What it is and why you need it | Cost | Instead, or can you skip it? | Room to choose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Claude Code | A program that lets an AI model work with files on your own computer and run small programs. The full version's skills (a folder of written instructions an AI model can follow) run in it. See [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md). | See the current plans at https://claude.com/pricing. | You can use the same skills' instructions in a Claude or ChatGPT Project and skip the scripts. | A helper can run it for you. |
| Python (a programming language), version 3 | The engine and two research scripts are written in it. It is a free download from https://www.python.org/downloads/. | Free. | Needed for the engine and scripts only. | A recent version. The engine was tested on 3.13. |
| A scheduler (the part of a computer that runs programs on a timetable) | The part of your computer that runs a program on a timetable (cron on Mac and Linux, Task Scheduler on Windows). The weekly sweep (a search that runs by itself every week) uses it. | Free; built in. | Skip it and run the sweep by hand each week, writing down that you did. | Pick a weekday and hour when the computer is on. |

## Optional, in any version

| Item | What it is and why you might want it | Cost | Instead, or can you skip it? | Room to choose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Funder directories | Paid websites such as Candid, Instrumentl and GrantStation that list funders and deadlines. | See each vendor's own pricing page. Prices change and differ by size of organization. | Skip them. A foundation's own tax filings and website show more, and the kit's method uses those. Many libraries and community foundations offer free access to Candid. | Ask your local library or community foundation first. |
| Online giving tools | A donation platform, a matching-gift search box on your donation form, or a stock-gift service. | Varies, and some charge a share of each gift. Read each vendor's pricing page, write down the date, and work out the cost on a sample gift. | Skip them. The free path in the giving-channels pack uses what you already have. | Decide only after you see which gifts you are losing. |
| Setup help | Someone to fit the kit to your organization. | Setup questions are free. Paid engagements cover the helper's time and costs, and no price is published. | Skip it. Everything is in this kit. | Ask at https://pigasus.group/contact. |
