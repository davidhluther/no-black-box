# GR-FR-03: Research a Funder

**What you'll have.** A filled row in your Funders tab and an honest tier, ask and next step for one funder, built from the funder's own pages and tax filings. **Time.** About 45 minutes for the first funder, 20 for later ones. **You need.** The tracker, the [paste-in prompts (messages you paste into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini)](../free/prompts.md), the [funder research checklist](../free/docs/funder-research-checklist.md) and a free AI model (a program that reads and writes text), such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.

If you get stuck, the check-up (a button or command that tests your setup) prints a support code (a short code that tells a helper which step failed and holds none of your information). The last box on this page says how to use it.

A funder is any person, foundation, company or government office that gives money to nonprofits. This guide's rule is simple. The AI model reads and sorts text you paste in. It is never your source for facts about funders.

1. **GR-FR-03-1.** Get ideas for where to look. Open a new chat with a free AI model and paste [Prompt](../glossary.md#prompt) 1 from the prompts file, with your own facts filled in. It gives you kinds of funders and search phrases, and no names. Try the phrases in a web search, and write the names you find on your own list.
   You'll know it worked when you have a list of at least five real organization names that you found on web pages yourself. If the AI model gave you names without being asked, ignore them and check them like any other, because it may have invented them.

2. **GR-FR-03-2.** Run the existing-funder check for every name before you call it a prospect. Search your Pipeline tab, past reports, board minutes and bank deposit records for each name and for odd spellings. Prompt 3 helps match spellings. Write where you looked in the "Where we checked" column of the Funders tab.
   You'll know it worked when each funder row says either "none found" or names a past or current relationship. A past or current funder gets that status, not "prospect".

3. **GR-FR-03-3.** Read the funder's own pages. Open the funder's website yourself. Copy the text of its guidelines or "apply" page and paste it into Prompt 2. Then open the funder on ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer (a free website that shows nonprofit tax filings, at projects.propublica.org/nonprofits) and note its assets and recent giving. A foundation's Form 990-PF (its yearly tax return, which lists every grant it paid) shows what it truly funds. Put each answer in the Funders tab, with the web address in "Funder website", today's date in "Date checked", and verified, secondary or unverified in the confidence column.
   You'll know it worked when every fact in the row has a web address and a date behind it. If a website blocks you with a "prove you are human" test (a CAPTCHA), stop. Never try to get past one. Come back later or use a different page.

4. **GR-FR-03-4.** Decide the tier and the ask, then add a row to the Pipeline tab if you will approach this funder. The tier (1, 2 or 3) goes in the Funders tab (the Rubric tab explains them). In Pipeline, type the Status using exactly one of the words listed on the Rubric tab, such as Prospect, and fill in the request, the fit scores and your honest chance of winning (type 40, not 40%).
   You'll know it worked when the three calculated columns at the right of the row (Tier, Fit, Dollars per hour) fill in. If a status word the Dashboard doesn't count is typed, the check-up shows E12. If the calculated columns show errors, it's E04.

5. **GR-FR-03-5.** Make it a habit. Once a week, spend 15 minutes. Run the check-up, look at the Dashboard, update statuses and deadlines, and pick the one funder to research next. Search Grants.gov (the US government's public list of grant opportunities, at grants.gov) by hand for your keywords, slowly.
   You'll know it worked when the Dashboard's "Deadlines in the next 30 days" matches what is on your calendar.

> **How to get help.** Write down the step ID where you got stuck (it looks like GR-FR-01-3). If your kit has a check-up button or command, run it and copy the support code it prints. Then send both to Pigasus Group at https://pigasus.group/contact. Setup questions are free.
