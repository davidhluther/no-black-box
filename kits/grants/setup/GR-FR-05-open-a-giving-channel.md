# GR-FR-05: Open a Giving Channel

**What you'll have.** One new way of giving, ready to publish, such as a donor-advised fund (a charitable account a donor holds at a sponsor, from which the donor recommends gifts) page or a monthly gift form. **Time.** About an hour for the text, plus whatever your website editor needs. **You need.** The templates in the free folder's giving-channels folder, the [giving channels checklist](../free/docs/giving-channels-checklist.md), and the person who edits your website.

If you get stuck, the check-up (a button or command that tests your setup) prints a support code (a short code that tells a helper which step failed and holds none of your information). The last box on this page says how to use it.

A donor-advised fund is the best first channel. It costs nothing, and larger donors already use it. Employer matching gifts (a donor's company matching their gift) come second.

1. **GR-FR-05-1.** Pick one channel to open this month. Read its section in channels-setup.md in the giving-channels folder. Start with the donor-advised fund page if you have no other preference.
   You'll know it worked when you can say in one sentence which channel you picked and who will publish it.

2. **GR-FR-05-2.** Copy the matching template (for example daf-year-end-email.md) into a Google Doc. Fill every bracket from your Settings tab and your Claims tab. Never invent a number, an account detail or a tax statement. Leave the tax disclaimer exactly as it is.
   You'll know it worked when no square brackets remain in the Doc except places a person must still fill, such as a brokerage transfer number.

3. **GR-FR-05-3.** Do the steps only a person can do, listed in the channel's section: creating any account, accepting terms, opening a brokerage account that can receive stock, and confirming legal wording with your accountant or attorney.
   You'll know it worked when each of those items has a name and a date beside it on the checklist.

4. **GR-FR-05-4.** Give the finished text to the person who edits your website, and ask them to publish it and link it from your Donate page.
   You'll know it worked when you can open the live page from the Donate page on a phone and the page shows your correct legal name, tax ID and mailing address.

5. **GR-FR-05-5.** Decide how you will record gifts from this channel. Read gift-records.md in the giving-channels folder and choose a short code for the channel (for example DAF).
   You'll know it worked when your donor database or a sheet has a way to tag a gift with that code.

6. **GR-FR-05-6.** Put a date on your calendar each quarter to give the board the one-table report from gift-records.md.
   You'll know it worked when the calendar entry exists.

> **How to get help.** Write down the step ID where you got stuck (it looks like GR-FR-01-3). If your kit has a check-up button or command, run it and copy the support code it prints. Then send both to Pigasus Group at https://pigasus.group/contact. Setup questions are free.
