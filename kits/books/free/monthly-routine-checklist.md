# Monthly Read-Only Routine

A checklist to paste into a Google Doc. About 45 minutes once you have done it twice. Every step looks and none of them changes QuickBooks.

All figures in the examples are invented.

## Before you start

- [ ] I have this month's bank statements for every account, from the bank itself.
- [ ] I am signed in to QuickBooks with a login that can see reports.
- [ ] I know the date I am reporting through (for example 09/30/2026).

## Pull the reports (guide BK-FR-01)

- [ ] [Balance sheet](../glossary.md#balance-sheet) as of the report date, [accrual basis](../glossary.md#accrual-basis).
- [ ] Profit and loss for the month and for the fiscal year to date, same basis.
- [ ] [General ledger](../glossary.md#general-ledger) for the month (only if the next section finds a problem).
- [ ] Aged payables and aged receivables, if the organization has bills or invoices.

## Compare to the bank

- [ ] For each bank account, type the QuickBooks balance and the bank statement balance into the Accounts [tab](../glossary.md#tab).
- [ ] Any Difference that is not zero gets one line in the Note column. If you cannot explain it, run the cash questions in `prompts.md`, section 2.
- [ ] Note the last month someone matched the books to the bank statement line by line (a [reconciliation](../glossary.md#reconciliation)). If no one has, say so in the report.

## Update the cash sheet

- [ ] Type this month's Cash in and Cash out on the Monthly cash tab.
- [ ] Update the Commitments tab: remove what was paid, add what is newly owed.
- [ ] Look at the [Runway](../glossary.md#runway) tab. Write down Free cash and Months of runway.

## Look for the usual trouble

- [ ] Any entry in [Opening Balance Equity](../glossary.md#opening-balance-equity) this month?
- [ ] Any bank account with no entries for more than 45 days?
- [ ] Any liability that has not moved in 90 days?
- [ ] Any manual journal entries? Who made them, and what backs each one?
- [ ] Any related-party payment (a board member, staff member, or their family or business)? Is the disclosure on file?

## Finish

- [ ] Fill in the treasurer report (`treasurer-report-template.md`).
- [ ] Save the pulled reports in a folder with the date in each file name.
- [ ] Write down one question for the bookkeeper or accountant.
- [ ] Run the [check-up](../glossary.md#check-up) button if the sheet looks odd, and keep the code.
