# Monthly Treasurer Report Template

Fill this in about 30 minutes each month from the QuickBooks balance sheet (a snapshot of what you own and owe), the profit and loss report (income and spending over a period), and the bank statements. Keep it to one page. Every figure in the examples below is invented.

Label each number as it goes in. "Books" means it came from QuickBooks. "Bank" means it came from a statement. "Estimate" means you worked it out, and the arithmetic belongs in a note.
