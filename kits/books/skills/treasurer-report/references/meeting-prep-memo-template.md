# Board Meeting Prep Memo Template

A private working memo for the treasurer to write a week before a board meeting. It lays out the decisions, deadlines, and open questions in one place so the meeting itself is short. All names, dates, and amounts below are invented examples.

Keep this memo for yourself or the finance committee. It names people and gaps. The board packet gets the one-page treasurer report instead.

## One section per topic

Repeat this block for each topic (bank access, tax return, balance sheet (a snapshot of what is owned and owed on one date), licenses, donor records).

**What we know.** Facts with the source next to each one.

**What we don't know.** The gaps. Name who or what can settle each gap. Mark any guess as a guess.

**The rule we already wrote.** What the bylaws or policy already say, with the section number.

**What I recommend.** One recommendation, in one or two sentences, with the reason.

**What the accountant or the bank needs.** A table with the item, whether it was found, missing, or partial, and who supplies it.

## Sign-off checklist before the treasurer signs a return

1. Read the whole draft return and every schedule, not a summary.
2. Confirm which form applies against the real numbers.
3. Tie revenue, expenses, and the balance sheet to the reconciled year-end statements.
4. Confirm the starting net assets equal last year's ending figure.
5. Confirm the officer and director list covers everyone who served, with their pay.
6. Re-answer the yes or no questions about new activities, sold assets, and related-party deals for this year.
7. Get committee review and board approval on the record, as your own procedure requires.
8. Sign the e-file authorization yourself. Do not send a signing PIN by email.
9. After filing, save the acceptance notice and the return in the shared drive.
