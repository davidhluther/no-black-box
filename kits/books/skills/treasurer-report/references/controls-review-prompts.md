# Financial Controls Policy Review Prompts

Paste-in prompts (messages for an AI model) for reviewing your organization's financial controls policy against its bylaws. Use a free or paid AI model. Paste the policy first, then the bylaws, then one prompt at a time. All numbers below are invented examples.

These prompts review words on paper. They do not look inside QuickBooks, and they do not give legal advice. Anything that touches state law or the tax code is a question for your attorney or accountant.

## Standing instruction (paste first)

```
You are helping a volunteer treasurer review a nonprofit's financial controls policy against its bylaws. Work only from the two documents I paste. Quote the section number for every point. If the bylaws or policy are silent, say "not addressed" rather than guessing. Separate three things: what must change before adoption, what can wait, and what is missing entirely. Do not give legal advice. Say who should answer each question: the board, the accountant, the bank, or an attorney.
```

## Who can move money

```
List every rule about who may sign checks, approve electronic payments, or open accounts. For each rule give the dollar threshold, the number of signers required, and the section. Then tell me whether anything in the policy actually enforces the rule (for example dual approval in online banking), or whether it depends on people remembering. Example: a bank pays a check with one signature no matter what the policy says.
```

## Separation of duties

```
Find every task in the policy that touches money: receiving gifts, depositing, recording, approving payments, signing, reconciling the bank statement. Make a table of who does each task. Flag any person who does both a task that handles money and the task that checks it. In a small organization the usual split is one person receives and deposits, a different person approves or signs payments, and a third person who does neither reconciles the bank statement.
```

## Committees and authority

```
Does the bylaws allow the board to create a standing committee, and how must it be created (for example by resolution of a majority of directors)? Does the policy give duties to a committee? Does anything in the documents I pasted show that the committee was created that way? If not, draft the one-sentence resolution that would create it.
```

## Conflicts of interest

```
Compare the policy's conflict-of-interest section with the bylaws' conflict rule. Does the policy loosen anything the bylaws require, such as disclosure, abstaining from the vote, or approval by disinterested directors? A policy cannot loosen the bylaws. If it does, quote both passages and suggest wording that fits the bylaws.
```

## Accounting method and reporting

```
Does the policy name an accounting method (cash or accrual)? Does it match the method on the financial reports and the filed tax returns I pasted? List any mismatch as a question for the accountant, not as a conclusion.
```

## Restricted money

```
Does the policy require restricted grants and gifts to be tracked separately? Does the way the books are actually kept (from the balance sheet I pasted) show separate tracking? List the gaps between policy and practice. A gap between policy and practice usually needs a practice fix, not a policy rewrite.
```

## Promises the organization does not keep

```
List every recurring review, audit, compilation, or filing the policy promises. For each, say how often, who does it, and whether the documents I pasted show it happened. Where it has not happened, suggest either budgeting for it or rewording the policy to say what the organization actually does.
```

## Gaps checklist

```
Check whether the policy covers each of these. Answer Yes, Partly, or Not addressed, and quote the section. 1) Online banking access and levels. 2) Debit and credit cards. 3) Payment platforms that pay out to the bank account, and who may change the payout account. 4) Who approves contracts. 5) Staff: whether they may sign, what they may spend, who approves timesheets. 6) Bonding or crime insurance for people who handle money. 7) Petty cash. 8) Record retention. 9) A year-end closing step with dates.
```

## Draft the motion

```
Write one motion the board could vote on to adopt this policy with the changes you listed under "must change before adoption." Number each change. Use the bylaws' own titles for officers. Leave placeholders in square brackets for names and dates. Do not include anything that depends on a fact you could not find in the documents.
```

## After any answer

```
What did you assume that is not in the two documents? Which of your points would change if the bylaws were amended after the date on the copy I pasted?
```
