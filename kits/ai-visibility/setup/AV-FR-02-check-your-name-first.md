# AV-FR-02: Check Your Name First

Time, about 30 minutes. Before you ask a single question, find out what else answers to your business name. Otherwise a look-alike can make you look more visible than you are, and you will not know. This is the check the kit calls a [preflight](../glossary.md#preflight).

1. **AV-FR-02-1.** On the Config tab, fill in `business_name` and `website_domain` in column B. Use the name as customers say it. For the website, write only the address, such as example.org, without https:// or www. Leave the rest for guide AV-FR-03.
   You'll know it worked when both cells hold your details, not the example text. If you are unsure what to write, it's E03.

2. **AV-FR-02-2.** Search for your name four ways in Google, one at a time. First the name alone. Then the name plus your town. Then the name plus the word reviews. Then the name spelled the way someone who only heard it would spell it. Look at the first two pages of each. Write down every other business, person, place, product, or everyday meaning that shows up.
   You'll know it worked when you have a short list on paper, even if it only says "nothing else found". If every result is you, that is a good sign. Write it down.

3. **AV-FR-02-3.** Record them on the SameNames tab. Delete the two example rows and add one row per look-alike. In column A write the name or word. In column B write what kind of thing it is (another business, an everyday word, a person, a place). In column C write where you found it. In column D write how to tell it apart from you, for example "different town, different trade". In column E write severity. Use High if it is in your trade or region or very famous, Medium if it is elsewhere or a very common word, and Low otherwise.
   You'll know it worked when the tab has at least one row of your own, or one row that says "none found" in column A. If the [check-up](../glossary.md#check-up) later says E11, this step was skipped or the example rows are still there.

4. **AV-FR-02-4.** Ask a free [AI model](../glossary.md#ai-model) the same question. Open a fresh chat in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Perplexity. Paste the first prompt from Part 1 of `free/prompts.md`, with your business name filled in. Compare what it says with your list. Add anything new to the SameNames tab, but do not add a business you cannot find a real web page for. AI models sometimes invent businesses.
   You'll know it worked when each name you added has a web page you opened yourself. If the chat refuses or asks you to sign in, it's E15.

5. **AV-FR-02-5.** Do the same with your competitor. Search the name of the one competitor you will track, the same four ways, and add look-alikes to SameNames. Repeat the AI question from step 4 for the owner name if you will use it.
   You'll know it worked when SameNames covers both you and your competitor. Run Help, Run check-up. E11 should be gone.
