# AV-FR-06: Read Your Results

Time, about 30 minutes. You will count what you recorded, clean it, and decide what you are allowed to say about it.

1. **AV-FR-06-1.** Go to the Summary tab. In cell B1 type your run label, such as `2026-11`. The table counts, for each product, how many questions were scored, how many named you, how many named and cited you, how many cited you only, how many crowded you out, and how many named nobody useful. Brand-check and [control questions](../glossary.md#control-question) are left out on purpose.
   You'll know it worked when the numbers add up to the questions you scored (20 per product if you used all of them). If a cell shows an error or zero when you know better, it's E21.

2. **AV-FR-06-2.** Clean the run. This is the noise check. Filter the Results tab by `same-name` in column E and read each answer. Decide, one by one, whether the answer is about you or about a look-alike. Fix the E column if you were wrong. Then filter column E for `n` and scan those answers for a garbled version of your name, such as a misspelling, or "a local salon" plus a detail that is clearly you. Change those to `y` only if you are sure, and put "garbled, checked" in L.
   You'll know it worked when you can say, for each product, how many mentions were really yours. Write down one line, for example "ChatGPT: 2 raw, 1 clean, one was a look-alike". Keep both numbers.

3. **AV-FR-06-3.** Know what you may compare. Open the Runs tab. Every run you want to compare must have the same [question set](../glossary.md#question-set) version, and must come from the same method (all by hand in the apps). If a version differs, do not compare. If you added a product, do not compare its numbers with earlier months. Start its own series. The [check-up](../glossary.md#check-up) reports E09 when versions are mixed.
   You'll know it worked when the Runs tab shows one version, or you have written down why you are not comparing.

4. **AV-FR-06-4.** Write three sentences. First, what the counts say, in counts and not percentages ("named in 3 of 80 cells"). Second, the one thing you notice, for example which question group is strongest. Third, one guess about why, marked as a guess. A first run is a [baseline](../glossary.md#baseline). It has no trend and you must not call it one.
   You'll know it worked when your three sentences could be read aloud to a friend without needing any other page.

5. **AV-FR-06-5.** Read the [citations](../glossary.md#citation). Collect the websites in column H and tally the ones that appear most. These are the sites AI products lean on for your questions. Open each and check that it exists. An [AI model](../glossary.md#ai-model) sometimes lists a plausible address that does not load. Note where you are missing from a site you could join, such as a directory or a review page.
   You'll know it worked when you have a short list of the most-cited sites and whether you appear on them.
