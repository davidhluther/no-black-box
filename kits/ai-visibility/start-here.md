# AI Visibility Check

You will end up with a plain answer to a question every business owner is starting to wonder about. When a customer asks ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Perplexity who to call, does the answer name you, link to your website, or send the customer to someone else?

You get a tracking sheet with 28 customer-style questions already written, a way to ask them in the free versions of those products, a place to record what comes back, and a button that checks your setup. You also get a way to spot visits from AI products in your website analytics, and a way to tell your business apart from other businesses that share its name. Everything in the free version costs nothing and needs no [command line](glossary.md#command-line).

## Why this kit exists

Most of how [AI models](glossary.md#ai-model) decide what to say about a business sits in a black box. Consultants and software tools will sell you a score, but you cannot see which questions they asked, what the model actually said, or whether the "mention" was your business or a different one with the same name. A hair salon or a small food bank should not need to buy a report to learn what a model says about it. Nothing about the method is hidden, and this kit shows all of it.

## Who it is for

- A small business or nonprofit that wants to know whether AI models recommend it, on a budget of an afternoon.
- A person who has been sold an "AI visibility score" and wants to check it for themselves.
- A marketer or consultant who wants an honest, repeatable method. It works the same for clients.

You do not need technical skills. Every term is explained where it first appears, and the [glossary](glossary.md) lists them all.

## What you will have at the end

- A Google Sheet with six tabs. One holds your details, one holds the 28 questions with your business type and town filled in, one holds the run log, one holds results, one lists [same-name](glossary.md#same-name) businesses, and one counts it all up.
- A [frozen](glossary.md#frozen) question set (frozen means you never edit a question after you first use it, so months stay comparable).
- A first month of results from four AI products, a [baseline](glossary.md#baseline) you can compare with next month.
- A check for same-name businesses (the official term is a "[preflight](glossary.md#preflight)"), so a look-alike does not inflate your numbers.
- A custom channel in GA4 (Google Analytics 4, Google's free visitor counter) that shows visits from AI products.
- A Kit help menu in the sheet with a "Run [check-up](glossary.md#check-up)" button that prints one [support code](glossary.md#support-code) if something is off.

## The honest limits

Across four products, 20 headline questions make 80 answers. That is a thermometer reading, not a ranking. Answers change from one ask to the next, and the products themselves change quietly. You will learn to measure that noise, and the kit refuses to compare two months that used different questions.

## Three versions

The free version uses free AI products, Google Sheets, and a small Apps [Script](glossary.md#script) (a built-in Google tool that lets a spreadsheet run your own instructions). Low cost adds one paid AI plan and a logging script that fills in rows for you. Full runs the questions through each AI company's own developer interface with your own keys, using Claude Code (a program that runs on your computer and does multi-step work for you). See [Choose your version](choose-your-version.md). You can switch later, and your sheet carries over.
