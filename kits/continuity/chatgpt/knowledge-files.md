# Knowledge Files for the Continuity Project

Upload these kit files to the Project's knowledge area (a Custom GPT allows up to 20 files). They teach the AI model the rubric and the templates.

1. `free/bus-test-questionnaire.md` holds the scoring rules and the five functions.
2. `free/runbook-money.md` is the money runbook template.
3. `free/runbook-operations.md` is the operations runbook template.
4. `free/runbook-access.md` is the access runbook template.
5. `free/examples-bus-test.md` shows an invented example so the AI model sees what a finished score looks like.
6. `fixes.md` lists problem codes in plain words.
7. `glossary.md` defines every term used.

Do not upload your own filled-in runbooks. They hold details about your business. Paste the one section you are working on into a chat instead, and remove anything that looks like a password.
