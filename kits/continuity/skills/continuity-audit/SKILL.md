# Continuity Audit

Works in: Claude Code, Claude.ai, ChatGPT. No script is needed. Everything is conversation and documents.

## What this does

It scores how well a business could run without its owner, then helps write the runbooks that raise the score. The bus test is the measure and the runbooks are the repair.

## Method

1. Read `references/rubric.md`. It holds the five functions, the three questions and the bands.
2. Ask the owner for a business name and what the business does. Do not ask for any password, account number or recovery code, and stop them if they start to paste one.
3. For each of the five functions, ask the three questions in order. Stop at the first no. Record the score. Then ask two short follow-ups. What would stop first if they vanished, and where does anything written down live?
4. Total the five scores out of 15 and state the band. Name the weakest function and the strongest. Say plainly that the score is a map, not a grade.
5. Agree a target score for each function with the owner. Not every function needs a 3.
6. Pick the two or three functions where a two-week absence would hit soonest, and offer to start there. Use the runbook templates in `references/`.
7. Interview for each runbook. Ask one question at a time in the order of the template. Write the owner's answers in their own words. Leave a blank where they do not know. Never fill a blank with a guess.
8. Finish by naming the two-week test. Someone who is not the owner does one task from the page while the owner stays out of it. Plan to score again after.

## Rules that never bend

- Score only what the owner tells you or shows you.
- Treat an unsure answer as no.
- Write down judgment as well as steps, because the steps are the easy part.
- A runbook never contains a password. It says where logins are kept and how to get back in.
- If the people function is the weakest, you may show what re-teaching costs. Cost per departure is onboarding hours times the loaded hourly wage. Label it a what-if unless the owner gave all three inputs.
- Report the score and stop. Do not tell the owner they are doing it wrong.

## Output

A short page with the total, the band, the five scores with one sentence each, the weakest function, and a numbered list of what to write first. Then the drafted runbooks, if asked.
