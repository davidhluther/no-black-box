# Choose Your Version of the Continuity Kit

Both versions use the same questions, the same score out of 15 and the same three [runbooks](glossary.md#runbook). They differ in who asks the questions.

| | Free (FR) | Low cost (LC) |
|---|---|---|
| What it costs | No purchase. A Google account is enough to start (checked 11/13/2026). | One AI plan. Claude Pro was $20 a month billed monthly (checked 11/13/2026). For ChatGPT, see the current price at chatgpt.com/pricing. |
| Who asks the questions | You, reading the [questionnaire](free/bus-test-questionnaire.md) | An [AI model](glossary.md#ai-model) inside an [AI Project](glossary.md#ai-project), one question at a time |
| How you score | Yes or no cells in a [Google Sheets](glossary.md#google-sheets) [tab](glossary.md#tab) that adds up the score | The AI model scores as you answer. You can still paste the answers into the Sheet |
| How runbooks get written | You fill the templates in Google Docs | The AI model interviews you and drafts them. You correct each one |
| Setup time | An estimate of one hour for the score, then an evening per runbook | An estimate of 30 minutes to set up, then one sitting per runbook |
| Privacy | Everything stays in your own Google account | Your answers go to an AI company. Read its data terms. Never paste a password |
| What it cannot do | It does not prompt you with follow-up questions | The AI model can skip a question or invent a step, so you check the draft |
| Full version | Not planned | Not planned |

## Why there is no full version

A full version means scripts, scheduled jobs or connections to your tools. This kit does not need them. The hard part of continuity is getting what is in your head onto a page, and software cannot do that for you. Where software helps, in wiring a runbook into the tools you already use, a person fitting it to your business does it better than a script.

## How to switch later

- Free to Low cost. Keep your Sheet and your Docs. Upload the templates to the AI Project and tell it your current scores.
- Low cost to Free. Paste the AI model's scores into the BusTest tab, and keep the drafted runbooks as ordinary Google Docs.

## Room to choose

Use the free version for the score and the low cost version only for drafting if you like. Use only the access runbook if that is the gap that scares you most. Not every function needs a 3, and the questionnaire asks you to set a target for each.

Each paid piece has a cheaper option in the [shopping list](shopping-list.md). To begin, go to [CK-FR-01](setup/CK-FR-01-run-the-bus-test.md).
