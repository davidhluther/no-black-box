# Money Runbook Template

This runbook (a written guide to how one part of the work runs, detailed enough for a stranger to follow) is for the money side. If you were out for two weeks, someone you trust could keep the money moving without calling you. Fill this in once at the kitchen table, then keep it current. Every blank you leave is a phone call someone has to make while you cannot be reached.

Do not write a password here. Write only where logins are kept, and use the [access runbook](runbook-access.md) for how to get back in.
