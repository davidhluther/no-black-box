# Example Bus Test (Invented)

This is an example only. The business is an invented small food pantry, "Example Food Pantry", with one part-time coordinator who does almost everything. None of this is real.

| Function | Q1 written | Q2 stranger could follow | Q3 someone else did it | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Money and billing | yes | yes | no | 2 |
| Operations and fulfillment | yes | no | | 1 |
| Customers and relationships | no | | | 0 |
| Suppliers and tools | yes | no | | 1 |
| People and knowledge | no | | | 0 |

The total is 4 of 15, which falls in the band "runs on one person".

## What the coordinator wrote

**Money and billing.** If I vanished, the grant reimbursements would stop being filed within three weeks. The steps are in a shared document, and the treasurer has read them but never filed one.

**Operations and fulfillment.** If I vanished, the Thursday distribution would probably still open, because volunteers know the routine, but ordering would stall. The ordering notes are half a page.

**Customers and relationships.** If I vanished, nobody would know which families need delivery or who the church partner contact is. It is all in my head and on my phone.

**Suppliers and tools.** The food bank order portal login is in my head. The delivery day is on a sticky note.

**People and knowledge.** Volunteers are trained by following me around for a month. Nothing is written.

## What the example business chose to do first

Customers and relationships and People and knowledge scored zero, and one volunteer departure would take the contact list with them. The coordinator picked those two plus access, wrote a one-page access [runbook](../glossary.md#runbook), and had the treasurer test it. The next scores rose from there.
