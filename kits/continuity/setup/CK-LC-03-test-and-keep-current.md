# CK-LC-03 Test and Keep Current

A page nobody has used is a guess. You will have a stand-in work from it, then score again.

1. **CK-LC-03-1.** Choose a stand-in who does not already know how you work and ask them for one hour. Tell them you will stay out of it.
   You'll know it worked when someone has said yes and you have a date.
2. **CK-LC-03-2.** Give them one task from each [runbook](../glossary.md#runbook), such as walking one normal cycle of work, finding where cash stands, and getting into one critical account. Watch without helping. Write down each place they get stuck.
   You'll know it worked when you have a list of gaps.
3. **CK-LC-03-3.** Paste one section of the runbook (with any secrets removed) into the project and ask Pretend you are a stranger covering for me for two weeks. List every place you would have to call me. Compare that list to what your stand-in found.
   You'll know it worked when the [AI model](../glossary.md#ai-model)'s list catches some of the same gaps. It will miss some. Your stand-in is the real test.
4. **CK-LC-03-4.** Fix each gap, then have the stand-in try again until a task finishes with no questions.
   You'll know it worked when they finish without asking anything.
5. **CK-LC-03-5.** Score again. Tell the AI model what changed, for example "Sam ran the money runbook and got stuck at the payroll login". Ask for the new total and band. Add a repeating reminder in your calendar to score again in three months.
   You'll know it worked when the score has moved or you can say why it has not. If it did not move, the work did not land.
6. **CK-LC-03-6.** Optional. Run the [check-up](../glossary.md#check-up) in your Sheet if you keep one, and keep the [support code](../glossary.md#support-code).
   You'll know it worked when the box says everything checked out. If not, the code and step ID point at the fix in [fixes.md](../fixes.md).

> **How to get help.** Write down the step ID where you got stuck (it looks like CK-LC-03-3). If your kit has a check-up button or command, run it and copy the support code it prints. Then send both to Pigasus Group at https://pigasus.group/contact. Setup questions are free.
