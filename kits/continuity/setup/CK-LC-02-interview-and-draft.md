# CK-LC-02 Interview and Draft

You will answer the [AI model](../glossary.md#ai-model)'s questions about your own business, get a score, and have it draft the [runbooks](../glossary.md#runbook) you pick.

1. **CK-LC-02-1.** In a new chat in the project, type Run the [bus test](../glossary.md#bus-test) on my business, then one sentence about what you do. Answer each question honestly. If you are unsure, say no.
   You'll know it worked when each of the five functions gets one score from 0 to 3 and a sentence of why.
2. **CK-LC-02-2.** Read the total out of 15, the band, the weakest function and the strongest. Ask What would stop first if I vanished, in each function? and write the answers down.
   You'll know it worked when you can say which function would hurt first. If the AI model scored a function without asking you, ask it to redo that one. That is E10.
3. **CK-LC-02-3.** Agree a target score for each function. Not every function needs a 3. Ask the AI model to recommend the two or three to write first, by risk, and tell it if you disagree.
   You'll know it worked when you have a short list with a target next to each.
4. **CK-LC-02-4.** Ask the AI model to interview you for the first runbook, for example Interview me for the money runbook, one question at a time. Answer in your own words, and say "I don't know" where you do not.
   You'll know it worked when blanks in the finished runbook show as "______" and nothing in them was guessed. A step you never said is E11.
5. **CK-LC-02-5.** Never paste a password, a one-time code, a recovery code or a full account number. Say where it is kept instead, such as "it's in our [password manager](../glossary.md#password-manager)". If you pasted one by mistake, delete the chat, change that password, and see E12.
   You'll know it worked when the finished runbooks contain no secret.
6. **CK-LC-02-6.** Repeat step 4 for operations and access. Then copy each finished runbook into its own Google Doc, and share it with your stand-in as viewer only.
   You'll know it worked when your stand-in opens each Doc from their own account.
7. **CK-LC-02-7.** Read each runbook line by line against how you actually work. Fix anything the AI model got wrong.
   You'll know it worked when nothing in it is a step you do not do. The AI model drafts, but you are the one who knows.
