# Shopping List for the Continuity Kit

Every item the kit uses, what it is for, what it costs and a cheaper way around it. Every account points to the shared [keys and accounts guide](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md), which explains where to sign up and how to keep a login private.

| Item | What it is | Why you need it | Cost | Cheaper or free option | Can you skip it? | Room to choose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Google account | A free login for [Google Sheets](glossary.md#google-sheets) and Google Docs. See [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md) | Holds the score sheet and the [runbooks](glossary.md#runbook) | Google says anyone with a Google Account can create in Sheets (checked 11/13/2026) | A work or school account you already have | Yes, if you print the questionnaire and use paper | Any account you control and could hand to a stand-in |
| The questionnaire | `free/bus-test-questionnaire.md` | The three questions and the scoring | Free, in this kit | Print it | No | Edit the questions to fit your trade |
| Score sheet files | `free/templates/BusTest.csv` and `Churn.csv` | Adds up the score and shows the band | Free, in this kit | Add the numbers by hand | Yes | Change the target scores to fit your plan |
| Runbook templates | Three `.md` files in `free/` | The pages you fill in | Free, in this kit | Print and write by hand | No | Rename or reorder sections to fit your work |
| A [password manager](glossary.md#password-manager) | A program that keeps logins behind one strong password | The access runbook points to it | Many have a free tier. See each vendor's page | Whatever your browser or phone already offers, if two people can reach it | Yes, but it is the safest way to share access | Any tool the business, not one person, owns |
| One paid AI plan | ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro | An [AI Project](glossary.md#ai-project) that interviews you | Claude Pro was $20 a month billed monthly (checked 11/13/2026). For ChatGPT, see chatgpt.com/pricing | A free [AI model](glossary.md#ai-model) with the prompts | Yes | Check how each company uses what you type |
| A stand-in | One person who agrees to run the [two-week test](glossary.md#two-week-test) | The only way to earn a 3 | Their time | A friend, a board member or a trusted employee | No. A score of 3 needs it | Choose someone who does not already know your work |
| A calendar reminder | A repeating event | To score again every quarter | Free | A paper calendar | Yes | Your own interval |
| Help from a person | A bookkeeper, a volunteer or David Luther, whose contact details are on the start-here page | To fit the kit to your business | Ask. Setup questions are free | The guides, the fixes table and the [check-up](glossary.md#check-up) | Yes | Ask for help with one step or all of them |

## What you do not need

- No software to install.
- No access to your accounts. Never give the kit a password.
- No technical background. A word you do not know is in the [glossary](glossary.md).
