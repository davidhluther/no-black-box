# Shopping List for Owner's Numbers

Every item the kit uses, what it is for, what it costs, and a cheaper or free way around it. Every account or key points to the shared [keys and accounts guide](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md), which explains where to sign up and how to keep a key private.

| Item | What it is | Why you need it | Cost | Cheaper or free option | Can you skip it? | Room to choose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Google account | A free login that opens [Google Sheets](glossary.md#google-sheets) and [Apps Script](glossary.md#apps-script). See [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md) | Holds the Sheet and the [check-up](glossary.md#check-up) | Google says anyone with a Google Account can create in Sheets (checked 11/13/2026) | A work or school Google account you already have | Only if you use the full version alone | Any Google account you control |
| Your sales [export](glossary.md#export) | A [CSV](glossary.md#csv) file from your [point-of-sale system](glossary.md#point-of-sale-system) | The raw sales for the month | Included with most systems. See your vendor's page | A weekly sales report you copy by hand | No | Square, Toast, Shopify, Clover and most others can export |
| Your bank CSV | A download of the month's transactions | Money in, money out and cash | Included with most banks | Copy rows from your online statement | No | Remove any account-number column before you use it |
| Your booking report | A CSV of appointments with status | Customers, no-shows and capacity | Included with most booking tools | Skip it if you take no bookings | Yes. The customer and capacity sections stay empty | Any tool that exports date, service, status and client |
| Spreadsheet template files | The six files in `free/templates/` | The Sheet's [tabs](glossary.md#tab) and [formulas](glossary.md#formula) | Free, in this kit | Type the [formulas](glossary.md#formula) yourself from [formulas.md](free/formulas.md) | No | Rename columns only if you also change formulas |
| Free [AI model](glossary.md#ai-model) | ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini free plan | Runs the paste-in prompts | Free tiers exist and change. See each vendor's pricing page | Skip it and use the Sheet alone | Yes | Any [AI model](glossary.md#ai-model) works |
| One paid AI plan | ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro | Needed for an [AI Project](glossary.md#ai-project) in the low cost version | Claude Pro was $20 a month billed monthly (checked 11/13/2026). For ChatGPT, see chatgpt.com/pricing | A free AI model with the prompts | Yes, if you stay on the free version | Choose the product whose terms you trust. Check how it uses uploaded files. See [keys and accounts](../../guides/keys-and-accounts.md) |
| [Python](glossary.md#python) | A free programming language that runs the scripts | The full version only | Free. See python.org/downloads | Use the Sheet instead | Yes, unless you want the full version | Version 3.9 or newer |
| Claude Code | A program that runs Claude on your own computer and can run scripts | Optional in the full version | See the current price at claude.com/pricing | Run the scripts yourself from a terminal | Yes | Optional. The scripts run without it |
| A folder on your computer | A plain folder called owners-numbers-data | Holds the four CSV files | Free | Your Documents folder | Yes, unless you use the full version | Any folder you can find again |
| Help from a person | A bookkeeper, a volunteer treasurer or David Luther, whose contact details are on the start-here page | To fit the kit to your business | Ask. Setup questions are free | The guides, the fixes table and the check-up | Yes | Ask for help with one step or all of them |

## What you do not need

- No accounting software. Your exports are enough.
- No bank connection. You download a file yourself.
- No technical background. If a word is new, it is in the [glossary](glossary.md).
