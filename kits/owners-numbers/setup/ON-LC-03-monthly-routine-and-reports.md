# ON-LC-03 Monthly Routine and Reports

You will repeat the page each month and ask the [AI model](../glossary.md#ai-model) for the three longer documents.

1. **ON-LC-03-1.** Each month, [export](../glossary.md#export) the new rows, update settings.[csv](../glossary.md#csv) with the new month_start, month_end, cash_on_hand and prior_cash_on_hand, and attach the four files to a new chat in the same project.
   You'll know it worked when the new page names the right month. If it reads the old month, it's E31.
2. **ON-LC-03-2.** Ask a pricing question in plain words, such as If I raise cuts by 5 percent, how much business can I lose before it stops paying? The AI model should compute the [break-even](../glossary.md#break-even) volume shift from your price and cost and call it a [finding](../glossary.md#finding).
   You'll know it worked when it shows the arithmetic and does not predict how many customers you will lose. A prediction without an assumption named is E32.
3. **ON-LC-03-3.** After three months, attach the three saved pages and ask for a quarterly report. The look ahead must be labeled [what-if](../glossary.md#what-if) and name the season beside it.
   You'll know it worked when the report has the headings What happened, One naive look ahead, Where the money sits and Decisions for next quarter.
4. **ON-LC-03-4.** Ask for an advisory letter of about 200 words from you to your partner, board or yourself.
   You'll know it worked when it ends with one question and does not tell the reader what to decide.
5. **ON-LC-03-5.** Ask for a 12-month roadmap. Give it two or three baselines you actually know. Any target you have not given stays "to set".
   You'll know it worked when blank targets read "to set" and no target number appears that you did not supply. An invented target is E32.
6. **ON-LC-03-6.** Once a quarter, rebuild one month in the free Sheet and compare it with the [AI Project](../glossary.md#ai-project). They should agree.
   You'll know it worked when the two pages match. If not, trust the Sheet and tell the AI model which number it got wrong. That is E30.
