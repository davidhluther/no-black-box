# ON-FR-03 Read Your Scorecard

You will tell the Sheet which month you are reading and look at the one-page result.

1. **ON-FR-03-1.** Open the Settings [tab](../glossary.md#tab). In column B enter your business name in row 2, the first day of the month in row 3, and the last day in row 4. Use a date format Sheets recognizes, such as 09/01/2026.
   You'll know it worked when B3 and B4 sit on the right side of their cells. A blank name is E04. Dates that sit on the left, or an end before the start, are E03.
2. **ON-FR-03-2.** Enter your cash on hand in row 5 (what the bank balance showed on the last day of the month) and the month before in row 6. Enter the minutes you could have booked in row 7 if you take bookings. For example, two chairs for 22 open days at 300 minutes a day is 13,200. Leave anything you do not know blank.
   You'll know it worked when column E (basis) shows actual beside rows you filled and [what-if](../glossary.md#what-if) beside rows you left blank. If the Scorecard says there are no rows in the month, it's E12. Check that your dates fall inside the month you typed.
3. **ON-FR-03-3.** Open the Scorecard tab. Read the first line, the verdict, and then the numbers under Money in and money out.
   You'll know it worked when the verdict says the month paid for itself or ran short, with a dollar figure.
4. **ON-FR-03-4.** Look at the basis column on the Scorecard and [Metrics](../glossary.md#metric) tabs. A grey italic row marked what-if means the sheet borrowed an example for one of its inputs. A [what-if](../glossary.md#what-if) is a practice run of the math, not a [finding](../glossary.md#finding) about you.
   You'll know it worked when you can point at one what-if row and name the Settings input behind it.
5. **ON-FR-03-5.** Turn one what-if into a finding. Pick a measure you care about, such as [break-even](../glossary.md#break-even), find its inputs in [formulas.md](../free/formulas.md) (price per sale, [variable cost](../glossary.md#variable-cost) per sale, [fixed costs](../glossary.md#fixed-cost)), and enter your own numbers in column B on Settings. Rows 8 to 10 already fill themselves from your [exports](../glossary.md#export), so check them first.
   You'll know it worked when the basis beside that measure on the Metrics tab changes to actual.
6. **ON-FR-03-6.** Print or save the Scorecard as a page. Choose File, then Print, set Scale to Fit to width, and pick Selected sheet. To share it, choose Save as PDF as the destination.
   You'll know it worked when the preview shows the Scorecard on one page.
7. **ON-FR-03-7.** Run Help, then Run [check-up](../glossary.md#check-up) again.
   You'll know it worked when the lines about Scorecard and Metrics say they show 0 error cells. Error cells (such as #NAME? or #REF!) are E10 on Scorecard and E14 on Metrics. A message that Settings [formulas](../glossary.md#formula) are missing is E13. Copy the formulas from [formulas.md](../free/formulas.md) back into column D and E.

> **How to get help.** Write down the step ID where you got stuck (it looks like ON-FR-01-3). If your kit has a check-up button or command, run it and copy the [support code](../glossary.md#support-code) it prints. Then send both to Pigasus Group at https://pigasus.group/contact. Setup questions are free.
