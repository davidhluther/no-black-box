# ON-FR-04 What-Ifs, Pricing and Reports

You will test a price change, ask the Sheet a [what-if](../glossary.md#what-if) question, and turn the month into the three report documents.

1. **ON-FR-04-1.** Test a price change. On Settings, enter your average price in row 8 and what one more sale costs you directly in row 9 if you want to override the automatic numbers. Then enter the change you want to test in row 26 (0.05 means five percent more) and leave row 27 blank.
   You'll know it worked when [Metrics](../glossary.md#metric) row 6, the price move [break-even](../glossary.md#break-even) volume shift, shows a percent and its basis says actual. A negative number is the share of volume you could lose and still come out even.
2. **ON-FR-04-2.** Add an assumption on purpose. Enter a volume change you expect in row 27 (-0.04 means four percent fewer sales). Read Metrics rows 7 and 8.
   You'll know it worked when both rows say what-if. They always will, because a guess about customers is never a [finding](../glossary.md#finding).
3. **ON-FR-04-3.** Read the other measures on the Metrics [tab](../glossary.md#tab). Rows 11 to 13 give [cost to serve](../glossary.md#cost-to-serve), rows 22 and 23 give [customer lifetime value](../glossary.md#customer-lifetime-value), rows 19 and 20 give [headcount payback](../glossary.md#headcount-payback), and rows 24 to 26 give [working capital](../glossary.md#working-capital) and [cash conversion](../glossary.md#cash-conversion). Each stays a what-if until its inputs on Settings are yours.
   You'll know it worked when you can say which inputs would turn one of them into a finding.
4. **ON-FR-04-4.** Draft a quarterly report. After three months, copy the Scorecard values for each month into [prompt 5](../free/prompts.md) and paste it into a free [AI model](../glossary.md#ai-model), or use the outline in [report-templates.md](../free/report-templates.md) in a Google Doc.
   You'll know it worked when the draft has the headings What happened, One naive look ahead, Where the money sits and Decisions for next quarter, and the look ahead is marked what-if. If the draft shows numbers that are not in your Sheet, it's E30.
5. **ON-FR-04-5.** Draft the advisory letter with [prompt 6](../free/prompts.md). It is about 200 words and ends with a question.
   You'll know it worked when the letter states one thing to look at and does not tell the reader what to decide.
6. **ON-FR-04-6.** Make your monthly routine. Each month, add the new rows to Sales, Bank and Bookings, change the two dates on Settings, update cash on hand, run Help, then Run [check-up](../glossary.md#check-up), and read the Scorecard. Also try a 12-month roadmap with [prompt 7](../free/prompts.md), leaving any target you have not chosen as "to set".
   You'll know it worked when the check-up says everything checked out for the new month.

If your kit has a check-up button or command, run it and copy the [support code](../glossary.md#support-code) it prints. Then send both to Pigasus Group at https://pigasus.group/contact.
