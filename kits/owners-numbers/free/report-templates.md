# Report Templates

Three documents that grow out of the monthly scorecard. Copy the headings, fill them from the owner's own numbers, and leave a blank wherever a number does not exist yet.

## Quarterly report

1. Title and the three months covered.
2. What happened. A small table of sales, money in, operating costs and net operating, one column per month, plus the direction (rising, falling, flat or mixed).
3. One naive look ahead, labeled [what-if](../glossary.md#what-if), with the season named beside it.
4. Where the money sits. [Cost to serve](../glossary.md#cost-to-serve), customer value, next hire and cash caught between paying and getting paid. Each one is a [finding](../glossary.md#finding) only when its inputs are real.
5. Decisions for next quarter. A table with the decision, the owner of it and the date.

## Advisory letter

A letter of about 200 words from whoever advises the owner (a bookkeeper, a board treasurer, a partner, or the owner writing to themselves). Four paragraphs.

1. The verdict for the month in one sentence.
2. The two or three numbers that moved, with the direction.
3. The one thing to look at, stated as an observation.
4. One question to think about before next month.

The letter never carries an advertisement and never states a what-if as a fact.

## 12-month roadmap

Four quarters. Each quarter has a small table of [metrics](../glossary.md#metric) with a baseline, a target and the reason it matters, plus one first action. A target stays "to set" until the owner has typed a number with their own baseline in hand. Pick two or three targets a quarter, not ten.

Good targets come from the metric library: [contribution margin](../glossary.md#contribution-margin), [break-even](../glossary.md#break-even) cushion, no-show rate, [capacity use](../glossary.md#capacity-use), gross [margin](../glossary.md#margin), cash cushion in months.
