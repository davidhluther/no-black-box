# Knowledge Files for the Owner's Numbers Project

Upload these kit files to the Project's knowledge area (a Custom GPT allows up to 20 files). They teach the AI model your layouts and rules. Do not upload your own exports as knowledge. Attach those to the chat each month instead, so they stay out of the Project's permanent files.

1. `free/metric-library.md` explains the 13 metrics and the basis rule.
2. `free/formulas.md` lists every formula, so the AI model can match your Sheet.
3. `free/report-templates.md` gives the quarterly report, letter and roadmap outlines.
4. `free/prompts.md` holds the paste-in prompts.
5. `fixes.md` lists problem codes in plain words.
6. `glossary.md` defines every term used.
7. `free/templates/Settings.csv`, `Sales.csv`, `Bank.csv` and `Bookings.csv` show the exact column layouts.
8. `free/examples/sales.csv`, `bank.csv`, `bookings.csv` and `settings.csv` give an invented example business so the AI model sees what a good export looks like.

Keep each month's real exports out of this list. Check the Project's file settings so the files you attach in a chat are not shared with anyone else.
